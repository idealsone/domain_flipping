      Create Your Account

      The A4 preview updates in real time. Zoom in or scroll to check every section. Use the colour swatches in Style to match your target company's brand.

      Click Fullscreen to see your resume at full size. Hit Export PDF to download. Your resumes are stored in the cloud — access them from any device.

      1

      2

      Fill the Three Tabs

      Role & Recipient sets the job details. Your Story is where you write your opening, evidence and closing. Details & Style sets your contact info, accent colour and font.

      Review Checklist

      Stage 3 shows a checklist — name, recipient, opening, body and sign-off. Green means complete; amber means something is missing.

      4

      Save & Copy

      Save Draft stores your letter in your browser. Copy Text copies the plain text to paste into job portals. Fullscreen Preview lets you inspect the A4 layout before downloading.

      Privacy Policy

      Effective Date: 1 December 2024

      1. Information We Collect

      We collect information you provide when you register (name, email, phone), information generated when you use the service (resumes created, ATS scans, cover letters), and standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages visited).

      2. How We Use Your Information

      We use your information to operate and improve CVTara, to send transactional emails (plan purchase, expiry, payout confirmations), and to send optional marketing emails only with your consent. We never sell or rent your data to third parties.

      3. Data Storage and Security

      Your resume data and account information are stored on servers in India. We use AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plaintext.

      4. Cookies

      We use strictly necessary cookies for session management and optional analytics cookies (Google Analytics). You may disable analytics cookies at any time via your browser settings without affecting site functionality.

      5. Your Rights
