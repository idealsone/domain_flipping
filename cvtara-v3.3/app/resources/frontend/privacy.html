Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026 · Launch draft requiring owner details and jurisdiction-appropriate legal review.

1. What CVTara processes

CVTara may process account details, profile preferences, resume and cover-letter content deliberately saved by a registered user, job-application records, purchase and entitlement records, support requests, consented feedback and product-usage events.

2. Resume uploads and parsing

The free public diagnosis is designed to read supported PDF and DOCX files in the visitor's browser. The candidate can review and correct extracted content before analysis. Registered users choose when to save document content to their account. TXT upload is not offered.

3. Payment information

Payment-card numbers, UPI PINs and OTPs are entered in the configured payment provider's secure interface. CVTara stores payment references, status, amount, plan and timestamps needed for access, receipts, support and accounting—not full payment credentials.

4. Optional external writing assistance

If the owner enables an external writing provider and the user explicitly requests a draft, relevant text may be sent to that configured provider. This feature is optional, all suggestions remain editable and users must verify every claim. The final policy must name the enabled provider and link to its privacy terms.

5. Analytics and marketing

The owner may enable first-party events and configured services such as Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Microsoft Clarity or web push. The live site must display consent controls where required and document retention, cookies and advertising use.

6. Retention, security and access

Account and business records are retained only for the configured operational, legal and support purposes. CVTara uses HTTPS, access controls, password hashing, CSRF protection, private storage rules and audit-friendly status records. No system can promise absolute security.

7. Your choices

Users may request access, correction, export or deletion of eligible personal data and may opt out of non-essential marketing. Some payment or compliance records may need to be retained as required by law.

8. Contact

Before launch, the owner must replace placeholders in Admin → Footer & Policies with the legal business name, address, support email and grievance contact.
