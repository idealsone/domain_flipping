01

Your application, one clear step at a time

Start in your dashboard. Complete Account & Purchases with your profession, experience level and category. Open Resume Builder, choose a template, add your content, save to your profile and review the complete preview before exporting.

02

Why choose CVTara?

CVTara brings template selection, resume editing, cover letters, requirement comparison and saved drafts into one application workflow. You control the wording, colour and layout. Use it to present your real experience clearly. No tool can guarantee an ATS score, interview, job or search ranking; “best” depends on your needs.

03

Build and save your resume

1. Choose a layout using profession, style or photo filters. 2. Fill personal details, experience, education, skills and optional sections. 3. Adjust colour, font and section order. 4. Select Save to profile. 5. Reopen it from Account & Purchases. A local draft is not a confirmed account save: check the saved message. Choose a simple text layout when the employer requests it; use creative or photo layouts when appropriate for the role.

04

Create a matching cover letter

Open Cover Letters and choose a design. Enter the company, role and recipient, then write an opening, evidence of relevant work and a concise closing. Use facts from your resume and explain their relevance. Save the letter, inspect the full preview and check names and dates before downloading.

05

Understand your resume match

Add your resume and the target job description. Check imported text before analysis; scanned PDFs may need text copied from an OCR tool. Review each requirement, the supporting resume evidence, missing evidence and suggested edits. A missing term is not proof of missing ability. Add only skills and achievements you can support. Language differences, vague job descriptions and extraction errors can affect the result.

06

Preview, purchases and downloads

Check your active plan, purchase history, expiry and remaining limits in Account & Purchases. Previews may carry a watermark. A clean export uses the allowance shown for your plan. Preview selected layout preserves the chosen design for printing; compact PDF is a simplified layout, and Word is editable. Inspect every exported page. A pending payment is not a confirmed purchase.

07

Referrals, wallet and history

Open Referrals in the dashboard to copy your personal link and share it. Disclose that you may earn rewards or commission. Each qualifying friend must register through your link, verify their account and successfully pay for a plan. Sharing a link or collecting signups alone does not qualify.

View the current milestones and commission rates in Referrals. Each milestone is available once; the first milestone also counts towards the next. Wallet separates available, pending, reserved and paid funds. Referral history shows the progress of invited friends and purchase commissions. Failed, test and refunded purchases do not qualify.

Open Account & Purchases to claim an eligible reward, apply as a creator or manage payout details. Creator rates need owner approval. The owner may update programme terms for future earnings; existing commission records retain their recorded rates.

08

Set up a verified payout profile

Verify an email code with your current password. Submit legal name, Indian phone, address, your bank account and IFSC or UPI ID, and PAN if required. The owner reviews identity, destination ownership, referral activity and applicable tax treatment. New or changed details have a 48-hour safety period. Withdraw at least ₹1,000 from available funds; only one open request is allowed. The 24-hour payout target is not a guarantee. Never share your UPI PIN, bank password or security code with anyone.

09

Support and your content

CVTara’s interface and guides are in English. Your original resume and cover-letter text is preserved. If a save or import fails, keep your original file, note the error and contact support. Never send your password or a verification code.
