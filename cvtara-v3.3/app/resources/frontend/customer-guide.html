📝Registration

Anyone can sign up for free, build a resume and preview it. You pay only when you want a clean download.

  1. Open RegisterOn cvtara.com choose Register. Enter your name, email and a long password, and say whether you are a fresher or experienced.
  2. Confirm your emailWe email you a short code. Type it in to confirm the address. Your account is ready once the email is confirmed.
  3. Sign inUse the same email and password at Sign in. Forgot it? Choose Forgot password and we email you a reset link.
We never ask for your password, UPI PIN or OTP by phone, chat or email. Anyone who does is not CVTara.

▤Resume creation

Your first resume is your primary resume: the master copy with all your real experience. Job versions are made from it.

  1. Choose a templateOpen Resume Builder and pick a template. Single-column layouts are safest for job portals.
  2. Fill each sectionContact details, summary, experience, education, skills and any projects or certificates. Use numbers where you can (for example "cut report time by 30%").
  3. Save to profilePress Save to profile and wait for the "Saved" message. A draft that only sits in your browser is not saved to your account.
  4. Change the look any timeSwitch template, colour, font and section order without retyping.
Only write what you can explain in an interview. Recruiters ask about every line.

📤Resume upload

In My Resumes choose Upload your resume and pick a Word, PDF or text file. We read it and fill the builder: contact details, jobs, education and skills.

  1. Check every sectionReading a file is never perfect, especially two-column or image-based resumes. Read each section and correct what moved.

✨Resume improvement

Every resume shows an ATS-friendly score. It shows how complete and easy to read your resume is for job portals. It is a guide to improve your resume, not a promise of a shortlist or interview.

What affects this score?

Opens a list of exactly what is missing or weak, such as a summary, dates or measurable results.

Check my writing

Finds spelling and grammar slips in your resume text before you download.

Does CVTara guarantee a 70% score or a job?

No. The score depends on what you add, and employers decide shortlists. CVTara shows what to fix; no score or result is promised.

Job versions

For a particular job, make a tailored version (see ATS matching). Your primary resume stays as it is.

◎ATS matching

ATS Match shows how well a resume fits one job and what is missing, so you can fix it before applying.

  1. Choose your resumePick one of your saved resumes from the list (your primary resume is first).
  2. Add the jobPaste the job description, or upload it as a PDF, Word or text file. A job-portal link can be read only when the page is public; for postings behind a login, paste the text.
  3. Read the reportYou get a match estimate, the job's keywords you have and miss, and what to fix.
  4. Build what you needMake a tailored resume, a cover letter or both from the report. The job is added to your Applications.
Each check uses 1 ATS scan from your package; a check that cannot finish uses none. Free accounts get a few checks a day.
Add a missing keyword only when it is true for you. A matching score is a guide, not a promise of a shortlist.

✉️Cover-letter creation

A short letter that connects your evidence to the role helps for email applications and roles that ask for one.

  1. Open Cover LettersPick a design, then enter the company, the role and, if you know it, the person to address.
  2. Write it from your resumePress Write my letter to get a first draft from your saved resume and the job, or write your own. Edit every line so it sounds like you.
  3. SaveSaved letters are listed under My saved cover letters.
Keep it to about 200 to 300 words in three short paragraphs.

🧩Combined service

From the Dashboard choose the tile for a resume and cover letter for one job. CVTara prepares both for you, step by step.

  1. Start the requestChoose your resume and paste the job. You can have one request running at a time.
  2. Follow the stepsThe tracker shows each step: tailoring the resume, writing the letter, checks, and the ATS check. Each step usually takes under a minute.
  3. PreviewWhen it is ready you see a preview of both documents and the match result.
  4. Save and downloadSave them as new versions (or update the ones you have). Downloads use your package as usual; if you need a package, the order summary shows the price first. One-time payment, no automatic renewal.
If a step fails, press Try again. Nothing is charged for a step that did not finish.

◇Interview practice

Interview practice helps you prepare for interviews in customer support, sales, HR, accounts, operations, retail and other non-technical roles.

Find your role

Type your job title, such as telecaller or front desk executive, and pick the matching role.

Research a company

Paste the company's own website. We read that one public page and give you practice questions about the company. They are practice questions, not the employer's real questions.

Timer and revision

Turn on the timer to practise answering in about two minutes. Revision shows questions from your past mock interviews with your earlier answer, so you can write a better one for free.

Practice questions

Pick a role and your experience to see common questions and what a good answer covers. Free accounts see a few questions per role; Job Hunt and Pro see them all.

Mock interviews

Answer a short set of questions in writing and submit. Written feedback, prepared automatically against a fixed checklist, shows what worked, what to improve and a stronger version of each answer using only what you wrote.

Full mock interview

Choose Full mock interview under Type for a round of about 60 minutes: four parts of about 15 minutes each (about you, the role, your past experience, situations and closing), three questions in each. A clock shows the time left in each part; you move on when you are ready and nothing is marked down for running over. It uses one mock interview credit, the same as a standard one, and the feedback shows how you did in each part.

How many

Job Hunt includes 4 mock interviews for its 30 days and Pro includes 12 for its 90 days, to use whenever you like. One is used when your feedback is ready.

If something goes wrong

If feedback cannot be prepared, or you do not submit within a day, the mock interview is given back. Think the feedback is wrong? Use Think this feedback is wrong? under it to tell our support team.

Your level (Beginner, Developing, Confident, Interview ready) is a practice guide only. It does not predict any interview result.

💬Ask Tara

When it is switched on, the Ask Tara button opens a chat that answers questions about CVTara, your plan and your documents, and can take you to the right page.

Tara also helps you write from your own text: clearer wording, achievement bullets, a profile summary, a version tailored to a job, a cover-letter draft, a check against a job, or likely interview questions. Paste your text (or ask Tara to use your resume) and copy what helps. Nothing in your saved resume or letters changes by itself, and each request uses one of your daily writing suggestions.

Tara can only see your own account. It cannot change your payments or your plan, and it never asks for passwords or payment details. For anything it cannot answer, it points you to Help & support.

Common questions

Do I have to pay to try CVTara?

No. You can sign up, build and preview for free. You pay only for clean downloads and bigger allowances.

What happens when my plan ends?

Your documents stay in your account. Clean downloads and package allowances stop until you buy again.

Is my data safe?

Your documents are private to your account. You can download all your data or delete your account from Account & downloads. See our Privacy Policy.
