    GOOD TO KNOW

    Quick answers before you start

    Short and honest. Still unsure? Ask our team, we reply on working days.

    Is it free to try?

    Yes. Create an account, pick a template, build and preview your resume, and run a few ATS checks a day for free. Free previews carry a watermark. Pay only when you want clean downloads.

    What do I get when I pay?

    Watermark-free PDF and Word downloads, your first primary-resume download free, ATS scans, tailored resumes and cover letters, the application tracker and reminders.

    What is a primary resume?

    Your master resume. Keep it up to date once, then create a version for each job from it in one click.

    When is a download credit used?

    Saving and editing are free. A credit is used the first time you download a clean file of a tailored resume or a cover letter. The same version in another format is free.

    Will it renew automatically?

    Never. You pay once for the days shown. Renewing early adds days to what you already have.

    What if my plan ends?

    Your saved drafts stay in your account. You can still edit them and download the watermarked preview. Renew any time for clean downloads.

    Are payments refundable?

    No. All payments are final and non-refundable, which is why you can build, preview and check for free first.

    Can CVTara guarantee an interview?

    No one honestly can. CVTara helps you present your experience clearly and match each role. Employers decide who they interview.
