Make your next move with confidence

What is a primary resume?

It is your premium, ATS-friendly master resume. You keep it up to date once, then create job-description-friendly variants from it for each application.

Do I need a plan to build a resume?

You can create an account, choose a template, edit and preview your resume first. A plan is needed for clean, watermark-free PDF and Word downloads — drafts and previews are free, previews always carry a watermark.

What do add-on packs do?

Add-ons add extra clean downloads on top of an active plan — for example +10 resume downloads and +10 cover-letter downloads, counted separately. They stay valid for 45 days from purchase and carry into a renewal inside that time.

When is a download credit used?

Saving a draft is free. A credit is used when you download a clean file of a tailored resume or a cover letter for the first time. The same version in another format is free; an edited version needs a new credit. Your primary resume is always free to download.

Do plans renew automatically?

No. You pay once for the access period shown. Renewing the same plan before it ends adds the days to what you already have.

Will I lose my drafts if I don’t pay?

No. Saved drafts stay in your profile within your account’s allowance.

Can CVTara guarantee an interview?

No. CVTara helps you present your experience and compare it with a role. Employer screening and hiring decisions vary.
