What is a primary resume?

It is your premium, ATS-friendly master resume. You keep it up to date once, then create job-description-friendly variants from it for each application.

Do I need a plan to build a resume?

You can create an account, choose a template, edit and preview your resume first. A plan is needed for clean, watermark-free PDF and Word downloads — free accounts can build 1 resume and 1 cover letter with watermarked previews; a plan unlocks clean previews, more drafts and downloads.

What do add-on packs do?

Add-ons add extra clean downloads on top of an active plan — for example +10 resume downloads and +10 cover-letter downloads, counted separately. They stay valid until your plan ends, and move to the end of the renewed plan if you renew early.

When is a download credit used?

Saving a draft is free. A credit is used when you download a clean file of a tailored resume or a cover letter for the first time. The same version in another format is free; an edited version needs a new credit. Your first purchase includes one primary resume download free, once for life; renewals do not add another.

Do plans renew automatically?

No. You pay once for the access period shown. Renewing the same plan before it ends adds the days to what you already have.

Will I lose my drafts if I don’t pay?

No. Saved drafts stay in your profile within your account’s allowance.

Can CVTara guarantee an interview?

No. CVTara helps you present your experience and compare it with a role. Employer screening and hiring decisions vary.
