01

Install and protect the application

Keep cvtara_app outside public_html. Upload only the public_html contents to the web root. Enable HTTPS, create a separate database user, then open /install.php and create the owner account. Back up the database and application encryption key securely. Confirm the installer is locked afterwards. Keep APP_DEBUG off in production. Test on a staging domain before accepting payments.

02

Read the owner dashboard

Use Business overview for users, purchases and activity. Review Users & access, Plans & payments and Analytics together. Internal visits are not Search Console search clicks. Import or connect Search Console to understand queries and pages. Do not treat demo data as customers or revenue. Customer and owner routes have different permissions; a customer must receive access denied for owner pages and APIs.

03

Publish content with clear ownership

In Footer & policies, enter the legal operator name, contact details, about text, privacy, terms and refund policy. In Blog & SEO, edit the complete article, headings, images, alt text, links and FAQs. Review the public page after saving. Use real reviews with publication consent; dummy reviews must not be published as testimonials. Check metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap and robots against the deployed domain.

04

Configure and test email automation

Configure SMTP or Resend and a verified sending domain, then enable email. Edit templates and triggers in Email automation. Marketing messages require consent. Configure the private cron runner every minute: security codes expire after 10 minutes, so the older 15-minute interval is unsuitable. Test signup, password reset, purchase, abandoned checkout consent, blog consent and access reminders. Check queued, sent and failed jobs plus the scheduler heartbeat. Never enable live email using a sample sender.

07

Referrals, wallet and history

Open Referrals in the dashboard to copy your personal link and share it. Disclose that you may earn rewards or commission. Each qualifying friend must register through your link, verify their account and successfully pay for a plan. Sharing a link or collecting signups alone does not qualify.

View the current milestones and commission rates in Referrals. Each milestone is available once; the first milestone also counts towards the next. Wallet separates available, pending, reserved and paid funds. Referral history shows the progress of invited friends and purchase commissions. Failed, test and refunded purchases do not qualify.

Open Account & Purchases to claim an eligible reward, apply as a creator or manage payout details. Creator rates need owner approval. The owner may update programme terms for future earnings; existing commission records retain their recorded rates.

06

Approve and reconcile withdrawals

First review the payout profile: legal identity, bank/UPI ownership, duplicate destination/PAN flags and applicable tax treatment. Record an evidence reference, not raw ID documents in notes. Approval checkboxes record a human review; they are not automated KYC. Details must complete a 48-hour safety period. For each withdrawal, review the checklist, mark processing, then choose manual payment or Cashfree. For manual payment, transfer first and record the real bank reference. Never send manually after an uncertain Cashfree submission.

07

Cashfree activation and acceptance

Payment Gateway and Payouts can use one Cashfree merchant account, but need separate product activation and integration credentials. Confirm freelancer eligibility and your affiliate-commission use case with Cashfree. The current checkout implementation uses Razorpay; adding Payouts credentials does not add Cashfree checkout. Payouts configuration is in Referral operations. Complete provider sandbox tests, configure IP allowlisting or the required public-key signature, fund the payout source and verify production permissions. This build blocks sandbox from settling real balances. Production submission is not payment success: sync must confirm the matching transfer, amount, completed status and UTR. Unknown results remain reserved.

08

Security and launch gate

Use strong unique owner passwords and Cashfree account 2FA. Keep secrets server-side, restrict server access, update dependencies and test backups. Review refunds, chargebacks, unusual referral activity and duplicate destinations before payouts. Decide PAN/KYC evidence retention, tax deductions and dispute handling with qualified advisers. Test customer isolation, blocked users, consent, mobile navigation, all supported language flows and payment failure/retry behavior on Hostinger. This release is not a penetration-test certificate or a guarantee against fraud. Keep live payouts off until acceptance tests pass.
