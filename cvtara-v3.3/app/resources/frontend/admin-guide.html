🔐Admin login and security

Sign in at your private owner address (not linked anywhere public) with your email and password. The owner sign-in also asks for a code sent to your mailbox.

  • Confirm before sensitive actions: refunds, plan changes, deleting users, keys and team changes ask you to confirm with a fresh code and your password if you have not done so in the last few minutes.
  • Authenticator app: in Settings → Team & security everyone can switch on authenticator-app codes for sign-in.
  • Staff accounts sign in the same way and see only what their role allows (see Workforce Management).
  • Never share this guide, the console address or any key in chat or email.

📊Dashboard

Overview is the first page after sign-in.

  • Business numbers: registrations, verified sign-ups, paying and first-time customers, orders, gross collections, refunds, net revenue, payment success, ATS scans, resumes and cover letters, visitors, support backlog, approvals and tasks. Choose Today, 7, 30 or 90 days. Each tile has "How it is counted"; "not tracked" means the number is unknown, never zero.
  • Owner alerts at the top: many failed payments, the AI service failing, approvals waiting more than 48 hours, overdue tasks, payment mismatches, campaign spend with no buyer. Each is also written once a day to the Error Log.
  • Payment reconciliation button: see Orders and payments.

Your daily routine (10 minutes)

  1. Overview: read the alerts and today's numbers.
  2. Approvals & tasks: decide what is waiting.
  3. Support Inbox: new requests first.
  4. Error Log: open entries; fix, then mark resolved.
  5. Setup & health (Settings) should say all systems are healthy.

⌘Command Centre

Press Ctrl+K (⌘+K on a Mac) anywhere in the console.

  • Search menu pages, settings, customers, orders and support requests. You only see results your role may see.
  • Type a question and send it to the Business assistant; any change it suggests still waits for your Confirm.
  • The side menu is grouped; each group opens and closes and the browser remembers your choice.

👥Customer management

Open Users. You can search, filter (normal, paid, expired, blocked), pick the joining dates and download the list.

Mark someone as paid (offline payment, gift, correction)

  1. Click Mark paid on the user’s row.
  2. Pick the plan, optionally change the days or the amount received, and write a short note.
  3. Keep “Email the customer a thank-you now” ticked and press Activate subscription.

Access starts immediately and the customer receives “Thank you for subscribing to CVTara …” with their plan, validity and a link to their workspace.

Other actions

  • Edit — name, email, phone, city, profession, level.
  • Manage (paid users) — add or extend a plan, or Revoke access with a reason.
  • Block / Unblock — signs the person out immediately.
  • Delete — erases personal data and documents; purchase records stay for accounting.
Sensitive actions ask you to confirm with an email code and your password. This protects the account if your screen is left open.

🛒Orders and payments

Orders shows every payment. Filter by status, plan, payment source, dates and amount, search by name, email or reference, and export what you see. Click any row for the full order: payment, customer, plan snapshot at purchase, timeline and emails sent.

7-day refund: customers can ask for a full refund within 7 days if nothing paid for was used, once per customer. Requests appear in Refunds & services; press Approve to send the money back through Razorpay (the gateway confirmation then ends access), or Decline with a reason. If a bank reverses a payment (chargeback) the payment gateway reports it and CVTara removes that access automatically. Use Revoke access on a user only for misuse. Coupons are managed in Business Ops; referral rules and payouts are managed only in Referral Ops.

Payment reconciliation

Every morning after 6 AM (India time) yesterday's captured Razorpay payments are compared with CVTara's orders, and open PhonePe orders are checked. Each mismatch (paid at the gateway but not recorded, recorded but not paid, amounts differ) is listed once in Overview → Payment reconciliation and in the Error Log. Look into it, then Mark checked with a note. Nothing is charged or refunded by the check. Finance can see it; only you can run it or mark items checked.

Packages and prices

Pricing Plans holds the package names, prices, days, allowances and perks: Quick Download ₹99 (1 resume, 7 days), Job Hunt ₹299 (10 resumes and 10 cover letters, 20 ATS scans, 30 days) and Pro ₹599 (20 and 20, 60 scans, 90 days). Changes show on the website, checkout and dashboards at once. One-time payments, no automatic renewal, 7-day refund if unused.

🎉Offers

Run a festival, early-bird or flash offer without editing any page. Open Offers & festivals:

  • Start from a festival: Diwali, Holi, Navratri, Ganesh Chaturthi, Pooja / auspicious day, Eid, Pongal, Onam, Christmas, New Year, Republic / Independence Day, Early-bird, Flash sale, Graduation season, Hiring season, or a blank offer. Change the heading, text, year, colours and picture. Type {{year}}, {{discount}} or {{code}} in any text and it fills in by itself.
  • Attach a coupon: pick an existing coupon or create one right there (percent or rupees off, plans, last day, total uses, uses per customer). The coupon is applied for the visitor at checkout.
  • One click to switch: Make live now turns an offer on and any other live offer off. Run by dates uses the start and end times (India time). A live offer wins over a scheduled one. An offer whose coupon is off or expired is hidden automatically.
  • Where visitors see it: a bar at the top of every public page, a pop-up after the delay you set, a different pop-up when the visitor is about to leave, and another when the visitor has gone quiet. Each can have its own wording. A visitor sees a pop-up again only after the number of hours you choose.
  • Clicks go straight to purchase: a visitor who clicks signs up (or signs in) and lands on checkout with the plan and coupon already applied.
  • Share it: Copy offer link gives an address that opens the offer pop-up and carries the coupon. Print coupon makes a printable card.
  • See what works: every offer shows how many people saw it, clicked, copied the code, and how many orders and how much revenue used its coupon.

Offers are shown on public pages only, never on checkout or inside the customer dashboard. Keep deadlines truthful: use a real end time or leave the countdown off.

🎁Referral operations

Referral Ops is the one place for the referral programme: the rules, each referral and its status, wallets, and any payouts.

  • A friend who signs up through a customer's link gets ₹50 off a Job Hunt or Pro package; the customer gets ₹50 in their wallet after the friend's refund window. Quick Download and add-on services earn no reward.
  • Monthly cap per customer and wallet expiry are set in Referral Ops.
  • Reviews (menu): approve or reject customer reviews. Only reviews given with consent can be published.

📧User communications

The mailer is always on once the support mailbox is connected. It sends the welcome email, subscription thank-you, access reminders, password resets and “your resume is ready — subscribe to download” to every customer. Edit any template, insert fields like {{name}} and preview before saving. Add SPF and DKIM for cvtara.com in Hostinger so messages reach the inbox.

Email campaigns

Send your own one-off email to many people, separate from the automatic welcome, receipt and reminder emails. Open Email campaigns:

  • Choose who gets it: all customers who said yes to emails, only those who have never bought a plan, those with a plan that is active now, those whose plan has ended, or your imported contacts. The number of people is shown before you send.
  • Import contacts: paste lines like email, name or upload a CSV file. Bad addresses and repeats are removed. You must tick the box confirming everyone on the list agreed to hear from CVTara. Someone who already has a CVTara account is reached through the customer groups instead, so their own email setting is respected.
  • Write the email: the same format as the other emails (# Heading, blank lines between paragraphs, - for a list, [button: Label | https://link]). Type {{name}} for the first name. The signature and unsubscribe link are added for you. Use Preview and Send a test to me before sending.
  • Send now or later: start straight away or pick a date and time (India time). Sending asks for your email code if you have not confirmed it in the last 10 minutes.
  • Safe sending speed: emails go out in small batches each minute and stop for the day at the daily limit (20 a minute and 300 a day to start with), then continue the next day. Raise the limits in Sending limits only if your email provider allows more.
  • Watch and control: each campaign shows how many emails are waiting, sent, failed, not sent and how many people unsubscribed. Pause holds back emails not yet sent, Resume carries on, Cancel stops it for good.
  • Unsubscribes: every campaign email has a one-click unsubscribe link that works without signing in. People who unsubscribe are never emailed by a campaign again. Add any other address to the Do-not-mail list yourself.

Campaign emails use your normal email settings and wait in the queue while email delivery is switched off in Settings.

Outreach and What's new

Outreach sends permission-based mail to imported lists from the chosen alias. What's new shows short release notes to customers in a pop-up and the bell.

⬇️Reports

Every list in the console (Users, Orders, User reviews, Support inbox, Email queue, Error log, Referral Ops, Outreach, Email contacts, the SEO checklist and Search Console tables) has a Download button next to its filters. Pick a Period (today, last 7 or 30 days, this or last month, this year, or your own From and To dates) and any filters first, then choose CSV (opens in Excel or Google Sheets), Excel (.xlsx) or PDF. The file holds every row that matches, not only the page on screen; Excel files also carry an “About” sheet listing the dates and filters used.

Reports & Export collects the business reports in one place: Daily summary (sign-ups, paid orders, revenue, ATS checks, resumes, page views, visitors), Pages visited, Traffic sources, Product activity, Orders, Users, User reviews, Support reports, Growth reports, Referral commissions, Withdrawals, ATS checks, Error log and Email queue. Choose the columns, then Download all rows. One file holds up to 50,000 rows; a PDF holds up to 3,000, so use CSV or Excel for anything bigger. The Download reports button on the Overview opens the Daily summary.

💬Customer support

Visitors who use the Contact page and signed-in customers who use the Feedback button in their portal send messages here (the Contact page is listed as “Contact form”). Customers send feedback from the Feedback button (what it is about, type, message, rating and up to 5 screenshots of 2 MB). Open Support Inbox to see new reports first, with the customer's name, plan, page and screen size. Open a report to read it, view the screenshots, set the status (New, Seen, In progress, Resolved, Closed), add an internal note the customer never sees, or reply by email. The menu shows how many are new. Screenshots are re-encoded and stored privately; include storage/app/support in backups.

Each request is a ticket with a status, an owner, internal notes the customer never sees, and replies. Customers follow their requests in My requests in their workspace.

👁️Assisted customer access

To see exactly what a customer sees, open their ticket and choose View as customer (read-only), with a reason.

  • Needs the "view as customer" permission. The session lasts at most 30 minutes and a banner shows the time left.
  • It is read-only: every change is refused by the server, and passwords, security codes, payouts and wallets are never shown.
  • Every page viewed is recorded with who, when and why; you can end any session.

🧭AI assistants

  • Business assistant (menu): ask about sales, sign-ups, customers or problems in plain words. Answers come from the database. Anything that changes data is shown as a card and runs only after you press Confirm.
  • Tara chat (menu): switch the customer chat on or off and read its limits. Customers never see the words AI or the provider's name; the site says "CVTara team".
  • AI service health: requests are counted; if fewer than 80% succeed over a day you get an owner alert. Each customer has daily limits, and a site-wide daily limit is set in Integrations.
  • Add or change the AI key only in Settings → Integrations; never paste it in chat.

🔎SEO Control Centre

Growth → SEO holds the SEO agent and every page record.

  • Pages: every sitemap page with Google's index status, impressions, clicks, issues and an audit score (24 technical checks). Filter by audit result; open Record for one page.
  • Page record: page type, keywords, intent, content status, who looks after it, the heading outline, image ALT coverage, links in, the call to action, the checklist and the audit history. "Scan and audit this page now" re-checks it.
  • Propose a change: a new title and description, main heading, FAQ questions, structured data, internal links or a content brief. Someone else with approve rights decides it in Approvals & tasks.
  • Content versions: each live change keeps its before and after, is checked on the live page about a minute later, and has a one-click Undo.
  • Editorial review: press "I reviewed this page today" after reading a page; the page then shows the review date. Never mark a page reviewed without reading it.
  • Site lists: orphan pages, pages missing from the sitemap, noindex pages in the sitemap and broken links.
  • Core Web Vitals need an optional Chrome UX Report key (Integrations → Analytics); without it they show "not tracked".
  • Scans pause while the site is in busy mode.

Blog and guides

  • Every guide and calculator has a readable web address like /guides/resume/action-verbs-list/, a focus keyword and advanced keywords.
  • The editor has a formatting toolbar, live preview, image upload, links, questions & answers and an SEO checklist that scores the page out of 100 (350+ words, keyword in title, description, first 100 words and a heading…).
  • sitemap.xml and robots.txt are generated automatically using your website address (https://cvtara.com).
Search indexing is off until you switch it on in Blog & SEO. Keep it off while you review the pages and switch it on at launch.

✅Approval Centre

🔌Integrations

Settings → Integrations holds every outside service. Keys are stored encrypted and never shown back (the page says "configured").

  • Payments: Razorpay and PhonePe keys, and which gateways appear at checkout.
  • Email: SMTP or Resend, sender name and postal address for email footers.
  • AI: the writing-service key, model and site-wide daily limit.
  • Analytics: Search Console property and service account, and the optional Chrome UX Report key.
  • Job reader: optional page-rendering service for job portals that refuse direct reading.

Speed and traffic

  • Ready-made pages: public pages are stored as finished files so visitors who are not signed in are served without the database. They rebuild when you save content. After uploading a new version press Rebuild all pages now.
  • Busy mode: Off, Automatic (when the server is loaded) or On. It pauses only ATS reviews, job-link reading and PDF or Word creation for a minute at a time with a polite message. Pages, sign-in, payments and saved work keep working.
  • Job link reader: most job links are read directly. For portals that refuse, add a page-rendering service key here (ScrapingBee, ZenRows or a custom address), set a daily limit and press Test the reader. Only the job link is sent. Check each job site's terms of use.

🛡️Security and backups

  • This guide, the owner console and the admin sign-in are never listed in search engines and never linked from public pages. Do not share these links.
  • Use a long unique password and keep your mailbox secure — it receives verification codes.
  • Never send the installation key or Razorpay secret over chat or email.
  • Each release is deployed only after a backup of the files and the database on the server.
  • Download a database backup to your own computer regularly and test that it opens.
  • Include storage/app (customer documents and support screenshots) in every backup.

Moving or reinstalling on Hostinger

  1. Upload the package to public_html.
  2. Open https://cvtara.com/setup/afsar — one private link. After setup it sends everyone to the home page.
  3. Enter the installation key, database details, owner login and the support@cvtara.com mailbox password. The installer sets everything up and locks itself.
  4. Sign in, add Razorpay keys and the cron job (every minute), then check Setup & health is green.

🛟Troubleshooting

Error Log and health

Server errors, browser errors, failed emails and daily warnings are collected automatically. Filter, download CSV/JSON, mark resolved. Setup & health runs a full check every day (database, storage, HTTPS, domain, cron, email, payments) and tells you exactly what to fix.

Common problems

  • Customer paid but has no access — open the order; if it is missing, use Users → Mark paid with a note.
  • Emails not arriving — User Mailer → “Send me a test email”; check the Error Log for failed emails and the SPF/DKIM records.
  • A page looks wrong — note the page and the button you clicked; check Error Log for a browser error at that time.
  • A customer quotes an error reference (12 letters and numbers): paste it into the Error Log search to find the exact entry.
  • A change does not show on a page: Speed & traffic → Rebuild all pages now.
  • An SEO change says "Not live": rebuild the pages, then press Check now on the page record.
