🚀Start here

CVTara works on one idea: every customer builds one premium, ATS-friendly primary resume, then creates job-description-friendly versions of it. Everyone who signs up is a normal user; a plan unlocks clean downloads, tailored versions and cover letters.

📅Your daily routine (10 minutes)

  1. Overview — check new members, paid users and revenue.
  2. Setup & health (Operations → Settings) — it should say “All systems healthy”. It checks itself once every day and keeps 60 days of history.
  3. Error Log — open entries; fix, then mark resolved. Download the log any time.
  4. Reviews — approve or reject new customer reviews.
  5. Orders — glance at today’s payments; click an order for full details.

👥Users & subscriptions

Open Users. You can search, filter (normal, paid, expired, blocked) and export to CSV.

Mark someone as paid (offline payment, gift, correction)

  1. Click Mark paid on the user’s row.
  2. Pick the plan, optionally change the days or the amount received, and write a short note.
  3. Keep “Email the customer a thank-you now” ticked and press Activate subscription.

Access starts immediately and the customer receives “Thank you for subscribing to CVTara …” with their plan, validity and a link to their workspace.

Other actions

  • Edit — name, email, phone, city, profession, level.
  • Manage (paid users) — add or extend a plan, or Revoke access with a reason.
  • Block / Unblock — signs the person out immediately.
  • Delete — erases personal data and documents; purchase records stay for accounting.
Sensitive actions ask you to confirm with an email code and your password. This protects the account if your screen is left open.

🛒Orders

Orders shows every payment. Filter by status, plan, payment source, dates and amount, search by name, email or reference, and export what you see. Click any row for the full order: payment, customer, plan snapshot at purchase, timeline and emails sent.

7-day refund: customers can ask for a full refund within 7 days if nothing paid for was used, once per customer. Requests appear in Refunds & services; press Approve to send the money back through Cashfree or Razorpay (the gateway confirmation then ends access), or Decline with a reason. If a bank reverses a payment (chargeback) the payment gateway reports it and CVTara removes that access automatically. Use Revoke access on a user only for misuse. Coupons are managed in Business Ops; referral rules and payouts are managed only in Referral Ops.

💰Pricing plans & download credits

Edit plan names, prices, validity, perks and add-on packs. Changes appear on the website, checkout and dashboards immediately.

ATS Match. Customers pick a saved resume from a dropdown (no uploads) and add a job by link, PDF/DOCX/text file or pasted text; links are read fresh on every check. Each check uses 1 ATS scan: Basic 20, Standard 40, Pro 60 (edit in Pricing Plans; add-on packs add 2 scans per resume credit and last until the customer's plan ends). A check that cannot finish uses no scan. After the report the customer builds a tailored resume, a cover letter or both; each clean download uses 1 credit of its own kind and shows a credit notice, and the job is added to the customer's application tracker automatically.

How allowances work. A customer's first purchase includes 1 premium primary resume download free, once for life; renewals do not give another, and nobody gets it without buying a plan. Customers can save drafts for free. A clean (watermark-free) download uses 1 credit of its own kind: resume downloads and cover-letter downloads are counted separately, so a customer who uses all 10 resume downloads still has 10 letter downloads (Fresher Basic = 10 + 10). Downloading the same version again in another format is free; an edited version uses a new credit.

Add-on packs (+10 / +20 / +30) add the same number of resume downloads and cover-letter downloads, kept separately, valid until the customer's plan ends (an early renewal moves them to the end of the renewed plan). They need an active plan. A stronger plan replaces a weaker plan's credits; add-ons stay on top.

Free users get 1 resume and 1 cover letter with a watermarked preview (account code, no copy/print) and must subscribe to download. Paid users see clean previews and clean downloads. No website can completely stop screenshots of a free preview; the watermark makes leaks traceable.

📄Resume & letter libraries

Click a card to preview the full document — the resume takes about 70% of the window and the details sit beside it (below it on phones). Edit changes name, category, accent colour or visibility. Customers’ saved documents are never changed.

🎨Website content

Templates & site design is pre-filled with what the website says today: headline, rotating job titles, buttons, announcement bar and the search title, description and keywords of every main page. Edit, save, and the website updates.

📝Blog & SEO

  • Every guide and calculator has a readable web address like /guides/resume/action-verbs-list/, a focus keyword and advanced keywords.
  • The editor has a formatting toolbar, live preview, image upload, links, questions & answers and an SEO checklist that scores the page out of 100 (350+ words, keyword in title, description, first 100 words and a heading…).
  • sitemap.xml and robots.txt are generated automatically using your website address (https://cvtara.com).
Search indexing is off until you switch it on in Blog & SEO. Keep it off while you review the pages and switch it on at launch.

⚖️Policies & URLs

Footer & policies holds your company details (CVTara · Bangalore, India · support@cvtara.com) and the About, Terms, Privacy and Refund pages. Press Auto-generate to rebuild them from your details, review, then Save. Below you will find every website URL with a checkbox for your final review.

📧User mailer

The mailer is always on once the support mailbox is connected. It sends the welcome email, subscription thank-you, access reminders, password resets and “your resume is ready — subscribe to download” to every customer. Edit any template, insert fields like {{name}} and preview before saving. Add SPF and DKIM for cvtara.com in Hostinger so messages reach the inbox.

🩺Error log & health

Server errors, browser errors, failed emails and daily warnings are collected automatically. Filter, download CSV/JSON, mark resolved. Setup & health runs a full check every day (database, storage, HTTPS, domain, cron, email, payments) and tells you exactly what to fix.

🎁Referrals & reviews

Referral Ops is the single place for the referral programme: every rule (commission, tiers, hold days, minimum payout, rewards, Pro-only), creator applications, payout identity checks, affiliate balances and withdrawal requests. Reviews lets you add reviews and approve/reject customer reviews — only reviews with consent can be published.

🎉Offers & festivals

Run a festival, early-bird or flash offer without editing any page. Open Offers & festivals:

  • Start from a festival: Diwali, Holi, Navratri, Ganesh Chaturthi, Pooja / auspicious day, Eid, Pongal, Onam, Christmas, New Year, Republic / Independence Day, Early-bird, Flash sale, Graduation season, Hiring season, or a blank offer. Change the heading, text, year, colours and picture. Type {{year}}, {{discount}} or {{code}} in any text and it fills in by itself.
  • Attach a coupon: pick an existing coupon or create one right there (percent or rupees off, plans, last day, total uses, uses per customer). The coupon is applied for the visitor at checkout.
  • One click to switch: Make live now turns an offer on and any other live offer off. Run by dates uses the start and end times (India time). A live offer wins over a scheduled one. An offer whose coupon is off or expired is hidden automatically.
  • Where visitors see it: a bar at the top of every public page, a pop-up after the delay you set, a different pop-up when the visitor is about to leave, and another when the visitor has gone quiet. Each can have its own wording. A visitor sees a pop-up again only after the number of hours you choose.
  • Clicks go straight to purchase: a visitor who clicks signs up (or signs in) and lands on checkout with the plan and coupon already applied.
  • Share it: Copy offer link gives an address that opens the offer pop-up and carries the coupon. Print coupon makes a printable card.
  • See what works: every offer shows how many people saw it, clicked, copied the code, and how many orders and how much revenue used its coupon.

Offers are shown on public pages only, never on checkout or inside the customer dashboard. Keep deadlines truthful: use a real end time or leave the countdown off.

💬Support Inbox

Visitors who use the Contact page and signed-in customers who use the Feedback button in their portal send messages here (the Contact page is listed as “Contact form”). Customers send feedback from the Feedback button (what it is about, type, message, rating and up to 5 screenshots of 2 MB). Open Support Inbox to see new reports first, with the customer's name, plan, page and screen size. Open a report to read it, view the screenshots, set the status (New, Seen, In progress, Resolved, Closed), add an internal note the customer never sees, or reply by email. The menu shows how many are new. Screenshots are re-encoded and stored privately; include storage/app/support in backups.

⚡Speed & traffic

  • Ready-made pages: public pages are stored as finished files so visitors who are not signed in are served without the database. They rebuild when you save content. After uploading a new version press Rebuild all pages now.
  • Busy mode: Off, Automatic (when the server is loaded) or On. It pauses only ATS reviews, job-link reading and PDF or Word creation for a minute at a time with a polite message. Pages, sign-in, payments and saved work keep working.
  • Job link reader: most job links are read directly. For portals that refuse, add a page-rendering service key here (ScrapingBee, ZenRows or a custom address), set a daily limit and press Test the reader. Only the job link is sent. Check each job site's terms of use.

🌐Moving to Hostinger

  1. Upload the package to public_html.
  2. Open https://cvtara.com/setup/afsar — one private link. After setup it sends everyone to the home page.
  3. Enter the installation key, database details, owner login and the support@cvtara.com mailbox password. The installer sets everything up and locks itself.
  4. Sign in, add Razorpay keys and the cron job (every minute), then check Setup & health is green.

🔒Security

  • This guide, the owner console and the admin sign-in are never listed in search engines and never linked from public pages. Do not share these links.
  • Use a long unique password and keep your mailbox secure — it receives verification codes.
  • Never send the installation key or Razorpay secret over chat or email.

🛟Troubleshooting

  • Customer paid but has no access — open the order; if it is missing, use Users → Mark paid with a note.
  • Emails not arriving — User Mailer → “Send me a test email”; check the Error Log for failed emails and the SPF/DKIM records.
  • A page looks wrong — note the page and the button you clicked; check Error Log for a browser error at that time.
