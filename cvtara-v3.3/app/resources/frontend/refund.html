REFUND POLICY

7-day refund if you have not used it

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. The 7-day refund

You get a full refund if you ask within 7 days of your purchase and nothing paid for has been used: no clean download, no paid ATS Match scan, no tailored resume; for Profile Boost, our team has not started; and for Tara Expert Review, the review has not been written yet. The Tara team starts on the review soon after payment.

2. Once per customer

Each customer can get one refund. A Quick Download that was credited towards an upgrade cannot be refunded separately.

3. How to ask

Open Account → Purchase history and press Request refund, or write to support@cvtara.com with your order number.

4. When the money arrives

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–7 working days. Access from the refunded package ends when the refund is processed.
