Completed digital delivery is non-refundable.

Last updated: 28 September 2026 · The owner must obtain jurisdiction-appropriate legal review before accepting live payments.

1. Digital access

CVTara products provide immediate or time-limited digital access after verified payment. Buyers must review the plan limits, duration, export allowance and feature access shown at checkout before paying.

2. When no refund is offered

Once paid access is activated, a clean resume or cover letter is generated or downloaded, or protected digital material is opened, the purchase is considered delivered and is non-refundable for change of mind, dissatisfaction, an unused balance, ATS results, interview results or employment outcomes.

3. Duplicate charge or non-delivery

If a buyer is charged more than once, charged incorrectly, or does not receive paid access after successful payment verification and reasonable troubleshooting, CVTara will investigate the provider record. A verified duplicate or incorrect charge, unresolved delivery failure, or refund required by applicable law may be corrected or returned to the original payment method.

4. Interview add-on

The interview-preparation add-on is non-refundable once protected resources are opened or manual review or coordination begins. Before delivery starts, only a verified duplicate or incorrect charge, unresolved non-delivery, or a right required by applicable law is eligible for review. Getting or not getting hired does not change refund eligibility.

5. How to request review

Contact the configured support email with the account email, order/payment reference, purchase date, plan and reason. Do not send a card number, UPI PIN or OTP. The owner should configure acknowledgement and resolution timelines in Admin.

6. Processing

Approved refunds are submitted to the original payment method. Bank or provider settlement time is outside CVTara's direct control. The account entitlement may be cancelled when a refund is approved.
