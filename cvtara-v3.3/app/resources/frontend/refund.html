PAYMENT POLICY

All payments are final and non-refundable.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. All payments are final

Every payment made on CVTara is final. We do not offer refunds, cancellations, exchanges, pro-rata credits or transfers, whatever the reason.

2. Decide before you pay

Check the plan, allowances, access period and price at checkout. By completing the payment you confirm that you accept this policy.

3. What a payment gives you

Plans are one-time purchases for a fixed period and do not renew automatically. Add-on packs end when the plan they belong to ends.

4. Questions

For help using your plan write to support@cvtara.com from your registered email address.
