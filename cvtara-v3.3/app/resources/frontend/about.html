About CVTara

From resume building to interview readiness. One career preparation platform for job seekers in India.

CVTara helps job seekers prepare more confidently for applications and interviews. We bring the essential tools together in one place, so you can move from understanding your resume to preparing for a specific job.

The problems we are working to solve

A resume is often your first introduction to an employer, yet many job seekers are unsure whether theirs is clear, readable or relevant to the role. Even when a resume is ready, tailoring it to each job post and writing a suitable cover letter takes time. Interview preparation is another challenge: it is hard to know which questions matter for your industry, role and experience level, and to practise answering them well.

How CVTara helps

  • Resume check and improvement. Your resume is assessed with a consistent, explainable method and practical recommendations. Scores are genuine; we do not lower or inflate them, and no score guarantees a job, a shortlist or an ATS outcome.
  • Job-specific matching. Paste a job post, upload it or share a public link. You confirm what was read, then see how your real experience matches it.
  • Cover letters. A draft built from your own resume and the confirmed job post. You stay in control and can edit every line.
  • Interview practice. Behavioural, situational and role questions chosen for your industry, role and experience level, with mock interviews that give you structured written feedback, prepared automatically.
  • Application tracker. Every job, date and follow-up in one place.

Preparation that reflects you

Not every job seeker needs the same guidance. CVTara uses your industry, role, target designation and experience level to keep resume examples and interview questions relevant. If your work does not fit the listed options, choose Other and describe it.

Our approach

We aim to make career preparation practical, transparent and affordable. We never invent skills, achievements, qualifications or work history, and we do not promise employment or a guaranteed ATS score. Plans are paid once and never renew automatically. Made by an HR team in Bangalore.
