1. The service

CVTara provides career-preparation software, including resume/job-description comparison, resume and cover-letter creation, application tracking, educational resources and separately described digital add-ons.

2. No employment guarantee

CVTara does not guarantee an ATS pass, interview, selection, offer, salary or employment. Match scores are explainable guidance based on user-supplied material, not access to an employer's private systems.

3. User responsibilities

Users must provide lawful, accurate information and verify every statement before sending a document. They must not use the service to impersonate another person, fabricate qualifications, upload malicious files, violate intellectual-property rights or attempt unauthorized access.

4. Plans and access

CVTara plans (Fresher and Professional tiers) and add-on packs unlock only the resume, cover-letter and ATS-scan limits and the access period displayed at checkout. Unless the owner deliberately adds recurring billing later with clear consent, the shared-hosting edition does not automatically renew.

5. Digital delivery

Access begins after signed payment verification. If money is deducted but access is not active, the buyer should contact support with the provider payment reference. Protected downloads must not be publicly redistributed.

If enabled by the owner and requested by the user, an external writing provider may process relevant text to suggest a draft. Email, payment, analytics and push providers have their own availability and terms. Suggestions may be wrong; the user remains responsible for the final application.

7. Interview-access add-on

Any interview-access listing must show current role facts, eligibility, location, schedule, inclusions and exclusions before payment. Preparation or coordination is not a job guarantee and employer decisions remain independent.

8. Suspension and availability

Accounts may be restricted for misuse, security risk, fraud or material breach. Maintenance and provider outages may affect availability. The final legal version should state governing law, liability limitations and dispute process for the owner's jurisdiction.
