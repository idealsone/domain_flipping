What your students get

  • Resume builder with role examples for Indian jobs, built for freshers and experienced people alike.
  • ATS check that compares a resume with the job post the student is applying to, and explains what is missing.
  • Cover letters written from the student's own resume, never with invented experience.
  • Interview practice by role, with written feedback on each answer and a progress view.
  • Application tracker so students keep every job, date and follow-up in one place.

How a partnership works

  1. Send the form on this page. We reply by email within two working days.
  2. We agree the terms in writing: seats, dates, price or reward, and what each side does.
  3. You get a campaign link and, if you like, a ready email for your students. Only students who sign up and verify their email through your link use a seat.
  4. You see sign-ups, active students and seat use in your report. We never share a student's resume or answers with you without that student's consent.

What we do not promise

No tool can guarantee interviews, shortlisting or a job offer, and we will not claim it. We do not sell student data or send students marketing they did not agree to. Plans never renew automatically.

Questions partners ask

Do students pay?

That depends on the agreement. Some partners buy seats for a batch; others share a link and students choose their own plan. Free accounts can build and check a resume.

Can we see each student's resume?

No, not by default. Reports show counts and progress. A student can choose to share a document with you.

Is there a minimum batch size?

No. Small coaching batches and large colleges both work. Tell us your batch size in the form.

Which languages are supported?

Documents are in English today, with guidance written for Indian job seekers.
