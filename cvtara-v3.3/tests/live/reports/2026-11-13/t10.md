# t10: speed and lag tester (customers[17], free plan)

## 1. Who I am and what I came to do
Speed tester on a laptop (Chrome, 1366x860, no throttling) and a Pixel 7 (Fast 3G about 1.6 Mbps, CPU slowed 4x).
I timed 12 key pages: when the first content and the largest content appeared, how much the layout jumped, when the main button could be clicked, and how heavy each page is. I also checked headers, caching, compression, source maps and what the APIs return.

**Read this first: what I measured and what I did not.** The laptop and Fast 3G runs ran 04:25 to 04:34 UTC, before the owner's scripted test run, so those numbers are clean. My three builder typing attempts ran around 07:46 to 07:50 UTC. That was during the owner's scripted run, so treat anything from that window as possibly affected. Not done because of time: the Slow 4G profile, the competitor homepages (Zety, Resume.io, Naukri), and the 30-second typing-lag test (see task log). The network throttle adds a minimum delay of 150 ms. Our real round trip through the test proxy was already about 350 ms, so the 3G figures mostly reflect the bandwidth limit, not the delay.

## 2. First 10 seconds
Public marketing pages are quick: home paints in 0.6 s on a laptop and about 1.0 s on a throttled phone, and nothing jumps. That feels good, better than most Indian job sites. The signed-in workspace is different. On the phone the dashboard **jumps badly about 7 s after opening** (layout-shift score 0.97, where under 0.1 is "good"), just as you would start tapping. Content shoves down under your thumb. I would stay, but I would be annoyed.

**Heaviest files** (same on most public pages):
- Fonts from Google: about 200 KB per page (Inter 84 KB + 48 KB, Fraunces 66 KB + 59 KB). That is the biggest single cost, about 45% of home's 437 KB.
- `/assets/bundle/*.css`: 36 to 49 KB (Brotli).
- `data.js`: 37 KB compressed, 120 KB raw. Loaded on home and on every SEO page.
- `/api/public-content`: 32 KB compressed, 93 KB raw. Includes the **whole blog index (61 KB) and the full privacy, terms and refund text**, and is sent `no-store`, so it is never cached. It is fetched on every guide page and every workspace view.
- `role-library.json`: 21 KB with **no Cache-Control header**.
- `cvtara-logo.webp`: 28 KB just for a logo. It is the largest file on /login and /register.
- Workspace: **105 requests and about 530 KB**, of which scripts are 226 KB across many separate files and API calls are 135 KB.

## 4. Problems (most serious first)
1. **Major: big layout jump on the signed-in dashboard (phone).** /workspace.html on the phone: CLS 0.97. The jump comes from `SECTION.application-roadmap` (0.72 at 7.0 s, plus 0.25), which renders late and pushes everything down. On the laptop the same page scores CLS 0.46 (`application-roadmap` and `tara-nudge`, between 4.6 s and 6.2 s). Screenshot: t10/workspace-phone-fast3g.png, t10/workspace-desktop.png. **Fix:** reserve the height of the roadmap and nudge cards (min-height or a skeleton) before the data arrives, or render them below the fold.
2. **Major: /checkout.html jumps on the laptop.** CLS 0.49, with a single shift of 0.41 from `DIV.co-grid` at 2.0 s. The plan cards and the selection panel re-flow after scripts run, so a user reaching for "Select…" can mis-tap. The same page on the phone scored only 0.01, so it is laptop-specific. Screenshot: t10/checkout-desktop.png. **Fix:** render the selected plan server-side, or give `.co-grid` a fixed column layout from the CSS.
3. **Major (needs a recheck): two 30-second first responses.** On the laptop pass, the guide page (`/guides/resume/achievements-bullet-points/`) took 29.0 s to first byte, and `/interview-questions/software-engineer/` returned a 164-byte "upstream request failed" page after 30 s. Screenshots: t10/guide-desktop.png, t10/interviewq-desktop.png. Repeat requests a minute later took 0.4 to 0.8 s, and the phone pass of both was fine. This could be the CDN (`server: hcdn`) waiting on a cold origin, or our test proxy. **Fix:** check the origin and CDN logs around 04:27 to 04:29 UTC for 30 s upstream timeouts, and warm or pre-render the SEO pages.
4. **Minor: builder template picker jumps.** On the phone, CLS 0.36: `studio-picker-tools` shifts 0.22 at 3.6 s, then the step navigation shifts 0.11. On the laptop, 0.11. Screenshot: t10/builder-phone-fast3g.png. **Fix:** reserve space for the picker toolbar and the template cards.
5. **Minor: SEO pages shift once on the phone.** The city/role page and the interview-questions page each have a 0.09 shift from `MAIN.sp-shell cv-page-enter`, an entrance animation that moves the layout. Fix: animate opacity or transform only.
6. **Minor: slow largest paint in the workspace views.** ATS Match LCP is 7.1 s on the laptop (4.9 s on the phone) and the dashboard 4.8 s, because the main text appears only after several API calls. Fix: render the shell text in the HTML and cache the session data.
7. **Minor: `/api/public-content` is fetched on every page and never cached.** It is 93 KB raw and includes the blog index and legal texts, which a guide page or a dashboard does not need. Fix: split it per page, and send `Cache-Control: public, max-age=300` with an ETag.
8. **Minor: too many separate scripts.** Home loads about 17 deferred scripts, and the workspace about 105 requests. Fix: bundle the JS the way the CSS already is.
9. **Polish: short cache lifetimes.** Hashed assets (`?v=hash`) are sent with `max-age=3600` only. They can safely be `max-age=31536000, immutable`, which would save return visitors many revalidation round trips on 3G.
10. **Polish: heavy logo and fonts.** The 28 KB logo could be an SVG or a 5 KB image. Use one font family (Inter) or self-host subset fonts, which would save about 120 to 150 KB per first visit.

## 5. Scores
- First impression: 7/10. Public pages paint fast.
- Look and feel: 7/10. Clean, but things move after load.
- Ease of use: 6/10. The builder is gated behind template choice before any typing.
- Navigation: 7/10.
- Speed: 6/10. Public pages are around 8, the signed-in workspace around 4 because of the jumps and slow largest paint.
- Trust: 7/10. Good security headers.
- Value: not judged (speed role).
- Mobile: 5/10. The dashboard jump is the worst experience I found.

## 6. Competitors
Not measured this round, because I ran out of time. I did not load Zety, Resume.io or Naukri, so I make no numeric comparison. What I can say: CVTara's public pages are fairly light (about 440 KB, 35 requests), and a slower first paint than about 1 s on a laptop would surprise me less here than on most ad-heavy job sites. **This is unverified.**

## 7. Would I pay?
Not my role, but a dashboard that jumps after 7 s on a phone hurts trust right at the upgrade card.

## 8. Best site for me?
For speed on public pages, it is good. The signed-in app needs layout stability work before it feels premium.

## 9. Top 5 changes
1. Reserve space for `application-roadmap` and `tara-nudge` on the dashboard, to fix CLS 0.97 and 0.46.
2. Give the checkout `.co-grid` a stable layout, to fix CLS 0.49.
3. Look into the 30 s upstream timeouts on the guide and interview pages, and warm the SEO pages.
4. Split `/api/public-content` per page and make it cacheable. Give `role-library.json` a Cache-Control header.
5. Use one-year immutable caching for hashed assets, bundle the JS, trim the fonts and shrink the logo.

## 10. Security
- **Mixed content:** none on any page measured.
- **Compression:** every same-site text file over 1.5 KB was Brotli-compressed.
- **Source maps:** `.css.map` and `.js.map` return 404, and no `SourceMap` headers were seen. Not exposed.
- **Security headers:** HSTS (1 year, includeSubDomains), a CSP (`script-src` includes `'unsafe-inline'`, which weakens it; scripts are allowed from Razorpay and Google Tag Manager), nosniff, SAMEORIGIN framing, a referrer policy and a permissions policy. Good, apart from `'unsafe-inline'`.
- **Third-party scripts:** only Google Fonts loaded on the pages I measured. No heavy trackers seen.
- **`/api/public-content`** (public, no login) returns legal texts, support and grievance emails, social links and the blog index. I found no customer data, tokens or secrets in it.
- **`owner-public.js` and `owner-console.css` are sent to customers** (and `owner-public.js` to the public), with `no-store`. I read `owner-public.js`: it only applies owner-edited content and calls public APIs (`/api/public-content`, `/api/traffic`, `/api/feedback`, `/api/allowance`). It contains no admin endpoints or secrets. The `owner-` name is confusing, and loading the owner-console CSS in the customer workspace wastes bytes. **Not a breach.**
- **Caching gaps:** `role-library.json` has no Cache-Control; `/api/public-content` is no-store.

## 11. What I did not like and who does it better
- The dashboard and the checkout jump after load. Mature builders such as Resume.io render their editor shell at a fixed size, so it does not move. Copy that: draw skeleton blocks with the final size.
- I cannot type anything until I pick and confirm a template. Resume.io and Zety (from public knowledge, not tested today) let you start typing with a default template. Offer "Start typing now, pick a design later".
