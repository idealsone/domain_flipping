## 4. Problems, most serious first

1. **MAJOR: the revenue totals include unpaid orders.**
   - **Where:** Orders and Reports & Export → Orders (`v-orders` text, `reports.png`).
   - **What happened:** "BILLED ₹805 / NET ₹805" is the sum of every row. That includes 2 orders with status `created` (checkout opened, never paid) worth ₹398. Real money received in the list is ₹407. The Overview says ₹99 for 30 days. So I get three answers to "how much did I make", and the biggest one is wrong.
   - **Fix:** NET should count only paid/success orders. Show "abandoned ₹398" on its own line. Make "Real money only" the default.

2. **MAJOR: "paid" counts don't agree between screens.**
   - **What happened:** Overview says "Paying customers 1" and "Paid users 1". Users says "PAID (ACTIVE) 10". Orders shows 9 ₹0 "success" orders that the owner recorded. Today's view says 0 paid orders while Orders shows 6 "success" orders today. Status reads "success" in Orders and "paid" in Reports. The same order is plan "Job Hunt" in Orders and "fresher-basic" in Reports (one Oct order).
   - **Fix:** One definition everywhere: "paying customer" means real money in. Label granted plans "Complimentary" or "Owner grant" and show them as a separate count. Use one status word.

3. **MAJOR: times are labelled wrong.**
   - **What happened:** The Overview stamp "Worked out at 04:26 am · India time" was UTC. Order times (04:22 am) are UTC with no label. The PDF header says "10:01 AM IST" while its rows are UTC. The CSV file name uses IST.
   - **Why it matters:** "Today" may also be cut at UTC midnight, which would be 5:30 AM in India.
   - **Fix:** Show all times in IST, and put the zone in every header.

4. **MAJOR: the Error Log shows a live outage risk, and nothing on the Overview tells me.**
   - **What happened:** The log has database "Operation not permitted" errors on `sessions` (×7, last 12 Nov 09:49), which means sign-ins fail while it happens. It also has "API key was refused" for the chat and business assistant (×11/×4/×3, 8 Nov), and the warning says customers saw "try again later". Yet the Overview tile "AI service success" says 100%, and there's no red banner.
   - **Fix:** A health strip at the top of the Overview: "2 open errors since yesterday → Error Log".

5. **MAJOR: the "View" button on a user does nothing.**
   - **Where:** Users row, "👁 View" (`user-view.png`).
   - **What happened:** Finding a customer is the most common owner task, and I couldn't open one. I couldn't see their orders, resumes, or a timeline.
   - **Fix:** A customer detail drawer with profile, orders, plan and expiry, tickets, and recent activity. That's what Shopify's customer page is.

6. **MAJOR: Ctrl K search is broken, and typing on the page fires shortcuts.**
   - **Where:** Header "🔎 Search Ctrl K" (`ctrlk-search.png`).
   - **What happened:** Ctrl+K opened nothing. The letters I typed then moved me to a different screen ("Business overview") with a different sidebar: an "Operations" group in place of "Customers & Orders". There are two navigation layouts in one console.
   - **Fix:** Make Ctrl K open a palette. Ignore single-key shortcuts unless no field has focus *and* the palette is closed. Keep one sidebar.

7. **MAJOR: the console is slow to become usable.**
   - **What happened:** The HTML is quick (0.4 s), but the page makes about 30 API calls on start. The browser "load" event took 16 s once, and more than 30 s on another try (my script timed out). The Business numbers card is still a skeleton at 8 s (`early-8000.png`). The SEO agent tab needs about 5 s more.
   - **Fix:** Load the Overview first and fetch the other views when they're opened. Cache the KPI tiles.

8. **MAJOR: an outreach unsubscribe rate of about 12% is shown but not flagged.**

   - **What happened:** "Outreach mails: 240 · Unsubscribes: 28". That rate puts the domain's email delivery at risk, and the console shows it as plain grey text.

## 10. Security
- **Signed-out access:** /admin.html → 302 to the home page. `/api/admin/overview`, `/purchases`, `/report?dataset=users`, `/me` and `/settings` → 401 "Sign in to continue". /admin → 302 to /admin.html, which then bounces. **OK.**
- **Owner password on the customer /login:** refused with a generic "Email or password is incorrect", and /api/admin/me stays 401. So the owner can't skip the authenticator code through the customer door. **OK.**
- **Wrong authenticator code (000000):** refused: "That code is not right…". I tried only one wrong code, so I didn't test the lockout.
- **Sign out, then browser Back:** stays on the public home page, and the admin API returns 401 afterwards. **OK.**
- **Session cookie:** HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax, 2-hour lifetime. There's also a 1-year `cvtara-device` cookie, which presumably remembers the device. Fine, but check it never skips the code.
- **Re-asking before sensitive actions:** I opened Settings (looked only), and nothing asked me again for the code. Approve, Mark paid, Delete and "Start counting from now" are one click from a session that is up to 2 h old. **Recommendation:** ask for the authenticator code again for money, deletion, team and settings changes, like Shopify and Razorpay do for payout and settings changes.
- **Secret owner sign-in paths:** both are plain first names and answer 200 to anyone. They aren't in robots.txt or the sitemap (good). Still, a first name is easy to guess. The code step is the real protection, so this is low risk, but I'd use a random path or rely only on /login plus the authenticator.
- **Error Log shows internal details:** database name, host and SQL. That's fine for the owner, but if staff roles can see the Error Log, hide them from staff.
- **A customer account opening /admin.html:** not tested with a customer password (per the rules). Signed-out behaviour and the 401s above suggest it's blocked server-side, not just hidden.
- **Writes:** I made none.
