# t07: accessibility and clarity tester (keyboard only, 200% zoom, night mode)

Screenshots: `tests/live/results/agents/t07/`. Account: customers[15] (free). Tools: Playwright (Chromium 1366x860), keyboard only (Tab / Shift+Tab / Enter). I also used axe-core 4.10.2 injected into the page, emulated `prefers-color-scheme: dark`, and set a 683 px CSS viewport at 2x scale to stand in for 200% zoom.
Handed in late (around 07:50 UTC; the deadline was 05:15) because the session hit a usage limit. All testing was done between 04:24 and 04:45 UTC, plus one sign-in and one security probe after the limit reset.

## 1. Who I am and what I came to do
I am an accessibility and clarity tester. I use the keyboard instead of a mouse, I read at 200% zoom, and I prefer night mode. I also look at the site through the eyes of someone whose English is weak.
I wanted to sign in, build a resume, run ATS Match on a real fresher job (Accounts Executive), and look at checkout, all without a mouse.

## 2. First 10 seconds
I liked the home page: it is calm, the type is large, the headline is clear, and "Free to build. ₹99 to download one resume. 7-day refund" is honest and simple (01-home.png). Every link shows a thick 3 px focus ring, which is better than most resume sites.
What annoyed me straight away: the first two Tab presses go to the Diwali "Claim this offer" banner and its close button. "Skip to content" only comes third (02-home-tab1.png). Yes, I would stay.

## 4. Problems, most serious first
1. **Major: focus is lost after every dynamic step in ATS Match and the Builder.** Where: `/workspace.html?view=match` (after "Run ATS check", "Check the saved version" and "Yes, check my resume") and the Builder industry chips. What happens: focus falls back to `<body>`, nothing is read out, and the "not saved yet" notice is not a live region. A screen-reader user does not know that a report appeared. Screenshots 26a, 26. Fix: move focus to the new heading (`tabindex=-1`) or put the status in `role=status`. The Builder's job step already does this well; copy that.
2. **Major: invisible elements take focus on the dashboard.** The closed Tara panel's textarea, "Send" and "Talk to our team" are Tab stops while hidden, and they are the first stops after load. Screenshots 09/10. Fix: hide the closed panel with `hidden` or `inert`.
3. **Major: no skip link inside the workspace.** It takes about 26 Tabs to reach "Upload my resume" and 38 to reach the name field in the Builder. Fix: add "Skip to main content", and move focus to the view's `<h1>` when a sidebar item is chosen.
4. **Major (clarity): obviously wrong email or phone is accepted silently, and the section shows a green ✓.** Builder › Personal details (19/20). A fresher will download a resume with a broken email. Fix: give a gentle inline hint such as "This email looks incomplete, check the @".
5. **Major (sign-in): the email is cleared after a wrong password.** Screenshots 06/07. People with weak English or motor difficulties must retype everything, and on retry they then see "email field is required". Fix: keep the old email value.
6. **Minor: the ATS Match empty-job error is far from the field, red only, not announced, and focus is not moved** (25). Fix: put it under the textarea with `aria-describedby`, `role=alert` and focus the textarea.
7. **Minor (clarity): the report wording is hard for weak English readers.** It says "requirements evidenced", "mentioned only", "keyword coverage", and shows two different scores that are both "6%" (ATS-friendly vs Match), which looks like a bug (26). Fix: use one big number and plain words: "You have 1 of 12 skills this job asks for."
8. **Minor: the job reader takes junk as skills.** HTML in the posting became "Good to have: `<b>bold<, b><script>…`", and "script" was counted as a matched keyword (26a/26). Fix: strip tags before extracting.
9. **Minor: overlays cover controls.** The "Ask Tara" floating button covers "Write a cover letter" (17/20). The price survey covers Back and the focused link (22). This fails WCAG 2.4.11 (focused item not hidden).
10. **Minor: aria-live is overused.** The whole template picker, the cover letter picker and the completeness box are live regions, so a screen reader would read out huge blocks on every change.
11. **Minor: contrast (axe).**
    - Home: green ✓ 2.27:1 and red ✗ 3.76:1 on white; the `.letter-sample-label` is 4.04:1.
    - Dark mode: `.tara-link` is 2.59:1; the indigo eyebrow labels are 3.67:1; the "Refer" button is 3.83:1.
    - Pricing: the About and Contact links can only be told apart by colour (1.3:1).
    - Checkout: the eyebrow labels fail.
    - Builder sample section headings are 4.48:1.
12. **Minor: focus rings are too faint on the login fields and the ATS textarea.** They use a 3 px blue glow at 16% opacity or a 1 px border, while everything else uses a strong 3 px ring.
13. **Polish:**
    - ATS Match "Paste text" / "PDF" are `role=tab` but each is a separate Tab stop (arrow keys are expected).
    - "Transport & Logistics" and "Transportation" are both offered as industries.
    - Placeholders such as "Bengaluru, India" are dark enough to look like already-filled answers.
    - Choosing "Accounting & Finance" still shows Product & Design templates first.
    - The dashboard says "0 / 0 free previews are watermarked", which is confusing.
    - The resume saved as "Untitled resume" even though the name was filled.
    - A stored session was signed out between runs, so I had to sign in again (seen once, may be expected expiry).

## 5. Scores
- First impression **8**: calm, honest pricing line, real skip link and focus rings.
- UI **7**: clean in light and dark; small contrast slips; floating buttons overlap.
- UX for keyboard / screen-reader users **5**: everything is *reachable*, but focus gets lost exactly when results appear.
- Navigation **6**: logical order; no workspace skip link; very long Tab chains.
- Speed **7**: views are about 4 s to settle; the ATS check took about 14 s with no announced progress.
- Trust **7**: plain-text handling is safe; admin APIs are refused.
- Value for money **7**: ₹99 for one download is fair.
- Clarity for weak English **6**: the buttons are simple; the report jargon is not.
- Mobile / zoom **7**: no sideways scroll at 200%, but sticky bars eat the screen.

## 6. Compared with competitors
These are my impressions of their public pages; I did not run an equal audit on them.
- **Naukri resume tools:** mostly forms; the focus styling is weak and there are many pop-ups. CVTara is clearly more keyboard-friendly and calmer. Naukri gives job listings in the same place, which CVTara does not.
- **Zety / Resume.io:** their builder field validation is stronger (a bad email is flagged as you type), and they autosave quietly without survey pop-ups. CVTara does better on honest one-time pricing (Zety's ₹/$ trial auto-renews) and on its dark mode, which Zety and Resume.io do not offer.
- **Jobscan:** its match report is jargon-heavy too, but it uses one headline score with clear "hard skills / soft skills" tables. CVTara's report should copy that single-score clarity. CVTara is better on price and on being India-specific (B.Com, Tally, GST were picked out correctly).

## 7. Would I pay?
Yes, Quick Download at ₹99 once my resume was ready, because nothing auto-renews. I would pay sooner if the ATS report used plain words and the builder warned me about mistakes before I paid to download.

## 8. Is this the best site for me?
Nearly. For a keyboard and night-mode user it is already better than most Indian resume sites. But a blind or screen-reader user would get lost the moment the ATS report appears, so it is not yet "the best" for full accessibility.

## 9. Top 5 changes
1. Move focus to, or announce, every new result: ATS report, job confirmation, unsaved notice, builder steps.
2. Hide the closed Tara panel from Tab, and add a "Skip to main content" link in the workspace.
3. Add inline validation for email and phone in the builder, and keep the email after a failed sign-in.
4. Rewrite the ATS report in plain words, with one main score and no "evidenced / mentioned only".
5. Fix the axe contrast failures (✓/✗ colours, `.tara-link` in dark mode, eyebrow labels, pricing links), and stop the floating "Ask Tara" button and the price survey covering buttons.

## 11. What I did not like and who does it better
- **Lost focus after results.** GOV.UK and Jobscan move focus to the result heading. CVTara should do the same.
- **No validation in the builder.** Zety and Resume.io flag a bad email as you type. Copy that.
- **Jargon in the ATS report.** Resume Worded uses short "Do this" lines and Jobscan has a single score. Use simpler English, and offer Hindi or Kannada later.
- **Pop-ups during the task** (price survey after the first save, Diwali banner as the first Tab stop). Kickresume and Novoresume keep the editor free of pop-ups. Ask the survey later, for example after the first download.
- **Sticky bars at 200% zoom.** Canva hides its bottom bar when you zoom or scroll. Hide the mobile CTA bar after the user scrolls.
- **Where CVTara already beats them:** a real skip link on public pages, a strong 3 px focus ring, a well-made dark mode, labelled fields everywhere in the builder, and a native modal dialog that keeps focus correctly.
