## 2. First 10 seconds after landing
- **Took 4.8 s to first content and 10.7 s until the page finished loading** on my slow 4G. For those 5 seconds I saw a white screen. On my real phone with Jio in a weak area, I would already be thinking of going back to Google.
- When it loaded (`01-home.png`): a huge orange "Diwali Dhamaka 2026" banner takes the top 10% of the screen. Diwali was on 8 Nov, so it is already over. That makes the site look not updated.
- The headline "One good resume, and a sharper copy for every job you apply to" is pretty, but for me, it is too much English. "Sharper copy" — I had to think. "Make your resume for free, download for ₹99" is what I wanted to read, and that line is hidden in small grey text under the buttons.
- "Built in Bangalore by an HR professional" — nice, I trust it a bit more because it is Indian.
- Two big buttons, "Start my resume" and "Check my resume against a job". Clear. Plus a sticky bar with "Check free / View plans" at the bottom. That makes three calls to action fighting for one small screen.
- **Would I stay? Yes, but only because ₹99 is cheap.** The look is clean and professional. The banner and the long sentence did not help.

## 4. Problems (most serious first)

### P1 — Template's fictional sample content is silently mixed into MY resume and downloaded — BLOCKER
- **Where:** Resume Builder → Choose a template → Freshers → Accounting & Finance → Accountant → "Use this design" → fill sections → Review → Download.
- **What happened:** After I chose "Fresher Campus Classic", the builder kept the sample person's content and added mine on top:
  - a fake **"Accountant Intern — Himgiri Finserv 2025–2025"** job with 3 bullets
  - a fake **"Final-year project — Team of four"**
  - a fake **"Certificate in GST — Online course 2025"**
  - fake **"B.Com, State University · First class 2022–2026"** (in addition to my real college)
  - **`linkedin.com/in/sample`** as my LinkedIn
  
  The preview has a tiny "FICTIONAL SAMPLE · REPLACE WITH YOUR FACTS" line. Still, the **"Content completeness 5 of 5"** bar and the **"ATS-friendly 86%"** badge both tell me it is ready.
- **The downloaded DOCX contains all of it** (`priya-watermark.docx`). A fresher like me would send a resume with an internship I never did. At an interview that is a lie, and that can get me rejected or blacklisted.
- **Screenshots:** `16-review.png`, `17-review-full.png`, `priya-watermark.docx`.
- **Fix:** When a user has their own data (or starts empty), apply only the **design**, never the sample content. If you want to keep sample text as guidance, show it as grey placeholder text that never saves or exports. Block download (or show a big red warning) while any sample entry is still there. "Content completeness" must not say 5/5 when the content is fictional.

### P2 — Very slow on slow mobile network — MAJOR
- Home: 4.8 s first content, 10.7 s full load. Login → dashboard: **39.9 s**. Builder: **19 s**.
- On every workspace load I counted duplicate API calls: `/api/allowance` ×4, `/api/resumes` ×2, `/api/referrals` ×2, `/api/downloads` ×2–3, `/api/resumes/originals` ×2. Each opening of the builder also POSTs a save of the resume, even when nothing changed.
- **Fix:** Call each API once per view and cache it. Send the dashboard first and load referrals, what's-new and feedback later. Indian freshers are on budget phones and patchy 4G. Naukri's mobile site opens the profile in about 3–4 s on the same network.

### P3 — Server errors (502) during normal use — MAJOR
- `502 GET /api/resumes` while moving through builder sections, and `502 GET /api/service-requests` on review. Nothing was shown to me, so I do not know whether a save failed.
- **Fix:** Look at the error log for these 502s. Retry once, and show a "Could not save, retrying" message when a save fails.

### P4 — Session quietly ended mid-task — MAJOR
- About 30 minutes after signing in, I opened ATS Match and got the plain "Welcome back" sign-in page (`22b-ats-before.png`). There was no "your session expired" message, and I was not taken back to ATS Match after signing in.
- **Fix:** Keep sessions longer on the same device (or add "Keep me signed in"). Tell the user why they must sign in again, and return them to the page they asked for.

### P5 — Pricing card contradicts itself (Pro) — MAJOR (trust)
- **Where:** Home → #pricing → Pro ₹599.
- **What happened:** The big numbers say **"30 Tailored resumes / 30 Cover letters"**, but the bullet under them says **"20 tailored resumes and 20 cover letters"**.
- Also, "Your first purchase includes one primary resume download free, once per account" — I could not understand this. If I pay ₹99 for one download, is the download free? Which one is "primary"?
- **Fix:** Make one number true everywhere. Rewrite the line, for example: "Any plan you buy also unlocks a clean download of your main resume."

### P6 — Free Word download watermark is only a footer — MAJOR for the business
- The free "watermarked" DOCX only has the text "CVTara · Preview" in the page footer (`word/footer1.xml`). The body has no watermark. Anyone can delete the footer in Word or Google Docs in 5 seconds and get a clean resume without paying ₹99. See Security.
- **Fix:** Offer DOCX only on paid plans, or put the watermark into the body (diagonal shape or header and footer on every page, plus a body text line).

### P7 — Template picker on phone — MINOR
- On phone, the industry list is ~30 pills, with duplicates ("Transport & Logistics" / "Transportation").
- Design cards are 2 per row with unreadable thumbnails, and the "Full preview" pill covers the middle of each thumbnail (`10-step3-designs.png`).
- The first "Use this template" button I hit gave me a UX/Product designer layout ("Prism / Creative") even though I was looking for finance fresher designs (`09-make-it-yours.png`).
- **Fix:** On phone, show 1 card per row, put the "Full preview" button under the thumbnail, and remove duplicate categories. After an industry is chosen, show only matching designs.

### P8 — Confusing words for a second-language English user — MINOR
- "0 / 0 free previews are watermarked · a plan unlocks clean downloads". "Make your next move." "CVTARA / RESUME STUDIO". "Content completeness" vs "ATS-friendly 86%" vs the ATS score. These are three different numbers and I do not know which matters.
- **Fix:** Use simple words, for example: "Downloads left: 0. Free downloads have a CVTara mark. Pay ₹99 to remove it." Show one score.

### P9 — Popups and the bubble cover content — POLISH
- The "Ask Tara" button and its greeting bubble cover the ATS card on the dashboard and the bottom-right of every form (`04-after-login.png`, `12-personal-filled.png`). The "is our price fair?" survey appears right after the first save, before I have paid anything.
- **Fix:** Make the Tara button smaller on phones and hide the bubble after the first visit. Ask the price survey only after a purchase or a download.

### P10 — Diwali banner still running after Diwali — POLISH
- "Diwali Dhamaka 2026 — Claim this offer" on 13 Nov, after Diwali (8 Nov). On phone it takes the top 200 px.
- **Fix:** Set an end date on the banner.

### P11 — Name typed into the builder appears twice in the DOCX — POLISH
- In the DOCX text, the name appears as a heading and again in the contact line ("Priya … Gowda · B.Com Fresher · …"). Check that this is intended.

## 6. Compared with competitors I would normally use
1. **Naukri (resume maker + profile).** Gives a free resume that is linked to job applications, which is what Indian freshers actually use, and it is faster on mobile. CVTara is better at design (real templates, clean PDF and Word) and at explaining what a job asks for.
2. **Canva (resume templates).** Free download with no watermark on free templates, and you can see the full design big on the phone. CVTara is better for ATS: Canva designs are often images or columns that portals cannot read, while CVTara has a "PDF · simple" format and ATS checks.
3. **Kickresume / Zety / Resume.io.** Kickresume has a permanent free tier with downloads. Zety and Resume.io charge about $2–3 for a trial that turns into a monthly subscription (₹600–1,800/month). Zety's builder starts with **empty** fields and grey "example" phrases you pick on purpose, so it never puts fake data in your resume. CVTara is better on price: ₹99 once, no auto-renewal, 7-day refund. That is honest and fits a student budget.

## 7. Would I pay?
- **Maybe ₹99 (Quick Download), not Job Hunt (₹299).** As a fresher I need one good resume, not 10 tailored ones.
- I would pay **sooner** if:
  - the preview showed only my own data (no fake internship),
  - the site loaded fast on my phone,
  - UPI payment of ₹99 was one tap and showed the Google Pay or PhonePe logos,
  - there was a "student / fresher" price or a free first clean download.
- Today, knowing I can delete the Word footer, a price-sensitive student will simply do that and not pay.

## 8. Is this the best site for me?
**Not yet.** The price and the honest no-subscription promise are the best I have seen, and the form is easy. But it almost made me send a resume with an internship I never did, and it is very slow on my phone. If P1 and P2 are fixed, yes, it would be the best choice for a fresher like me.

## 9. Top 5 changes I would make first
1. Never copy sample/fictional content into the user's resume. Templates should change only the look, and download should be blocked while any sample entry remains.
2. Cut slow-network load time: remove duplicate API calls, lazy-load referrals, what's-new and feedback, and target under 5 s to a usable dashboard on 4G.
3. Fix the Pro pricing contradiction (30 vs 20), and rewrite "first purchase includes one primary resume download free" in plain words.
4. Make the free DOCX watermark real (or offer DOCX only on paid plans).
5. Simplify the words for second-language users and show one clear score. Remove the old Diwali banner. Make the Tara bubble smaller on phone.

## 11. What I did not like and who does it better
- **Sample data in my resume.** **Zety / Resume.io** start with empty fields and show example phrases only as suggestions you click to add. CVTara should copy that: never prefill real-looking facts.
- **Slow first load on mobile.** **Naukri** and **Canva** open in a few seconds on 4G, with a skeleton screen straight away. CVTara should show a skeleton and load the extras later.
- **Template browsing on phone.** **Canva** shows one large template per row with a swipe. You can actually see the design. CVTara's 2-up tiny thumbnails covered by "Full preview" do not show me anything.
- **Too many scores.** **Jobscan / Resume Worded** show one clear match score with a short checklist. CVTara shows "ATS-friendly 86%", "Content completeness 5 of 5" and a separate ATS Match. Pick one.
- **Language.** **Naukri** uses short, simple English that freshers understand. CVTara copy reads like a design magazine ("Make your next move.", "a sharper copy"). Write for a Mysuru B.Com student, and consider a Kannada/Hindi toggle.
- **Payment trust.** **Naukri** and **Canva India** show UPI/GPay logos and the rupee price on every button. CVTara shows rupees (good), but the plan cards contradict themselves, which hurts trust.
