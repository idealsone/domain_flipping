Note: my session ran out of time part-way. The second half (Fix-my-resume, download, cover letter) was **not tested**. This review covers landing, sign-in, upload/parse, ATS match and security probes only.

## 1. Who I am and what I came to do
Rahul, Pune, 6 years of Java/Spring/Kafka/AWS. I am impatient. I have a resume already. I want to upload it, see if it was read correctly, and get a believable score against a real Senior Java Developer posting. Then I decide whether this beats Naukri's free tools and Jobscan.

- Landing page (`01_landing.png`) is clean and confident. The serif headline, "Upload the resume you already have... paste a job from Naukri, LinkedIn", the "Check my resume against a job" button and "₹99 to download, 7-day refund" all told me what it does and what it costs. - After sign-in, the dashboard (`03_after_login.png`) is busy:
  - The Tara chat panel pops open over the content by itself.
  - The page shows 3 counters, a "What brings you" question, 7 option cards, a 4-step "application workspace" and an "Ask Tara" button, all on one screen.
  - I just wanted "upload + check against job". It took me a few seconds to find "Improve my resume".

## 4. Problems (most serious first)
1. **Major - 429 rate-limit shows a blank white page** (`/login`, `02_login.png`).
   - The limit is about 240 requests/min per IP (`x-ratelimit-limit: 240`). One dashboard load fires about 20 API calls.
   - A whole office or campus behind one IP will hit this, and the visitor sees nothing at all.
   - Fix: raise the limit for page GETs and key it per session, not per IP. Serve a friendly "Too many requests, retry in N s" HTML page with a Retry-After header.
2. **Major - the ATS requirement matcher misses obvious evidence, so the 74% is not credible to an engineer** (ATS Match > report, `11_ats_report_full.png`):
   - It marked "Build event-driven systems using Apache Kafka" as **"Needs your review"**, but my resume says "Event-driven architecture", "Spring Boot and Kafka processing 2.4 million transactions/day".
   - "Design and develop microservices using Java 11/17, Spring Boot and Spring Cloud" - "Needs your review", but the resume says "migrating ... to 14 Spring Cloud microservices", "Java 11", "Java 17".
   - "Experience with AWS or Azure, Docker, Kubernetes" - "Needs your review", but all three are in the resume and the keyword chips are ticked green. The report contradicts itself.
   - "About the role: We are hiring a Senior Java Developer..." was treated as a *requirement*. The intro sentence is not a requirement.
   - "Project delivery - mentioned only" is a requirement the JD never asks for.
   - **NoSQL counted as missing** although MongoDB and Redis are in the resume.
   - **Oracle ticked as matched only because of "Oracle Certified Professional: Java SE"**. That is a false positive: the JD means Oracle DB.
   - "Preferred: React - not found", but Angular, GraphQL and OAuth2 (also preferred, also absent) are not mentioned. Inconsistent.
   - Result: 8 "missing / to review" items when the honest gap is about Azure, GitLab and Oracle DB. A senior dev reading this decides the tool is dumb.
   - Jobscan at least maps synonyms (MongoDB means NoSQL) and does not invent requirements.
   - Fix: match requirement bullets against keyword evidence already found, and add a synonym/umbrella map (NoSQL covers MongoDB/Redis/Cassandra; CI/CD covers Jenkins/GitLab CI). Skip intro/"About" lines. Do not count vendor names inside certification titles.
3. **Major - the profile is not updated from the upload**:
   - After importing a 6-year Java resume, My Resumes still shows *Field: Data & Analytics, Target job: Data Analyst* (`06_my_resumes.png`).
   - `/api/profile` still says `experience_level: fresher, experience_years: 0`.
   - So "your suggestions follow" the wrong field. Fix: after import, offer to set the field, title and years from the parsed resume.
4. **Minor - parse errors in personal details** (builder, `07_builder.png`):
   - Professional title is empty, although line 2 of the resume is "Senior Java Developer | Pune ...".
   - **LinkedIn URL truncated at the hyphen**: `linkedin.com/in/rahul-qa-test` became `linkedin.com/in/rahul`. That is a broken link on the final resume.
   - Education year (2018) and CGPA dropped.
   - The skills section became one blob that keeps "Frameworks:", "Messaging & Data:" labels inline but lost the "Languages:" label, yet the modal claims "30 skills".
   - "AWS Certified Developer - Associate" was split, with "Associate" put in as the issuer.
5. **Minor - the plan display flips**:
   - The sidebar first showed "Job Hunt, 30 days left" (`03_after_login.png`).
   - On the ATS report screen it showed "YOUR ACCOUNT: **Preview** - clean exports require verified paid access. See plans" (`11_ats_report.png`). The API said `plan: job-hunt, paid: true` throughout.
   - A paying user who sees "Preview" will panic about losing what they paid for. Probably a fallback after a failed call (I also saw a **502 on `/api/notifications`**).
6. **Minor - layout and display bugs on the ATS confirm and report screens**:
   - "Is this the right job?" card (`10_ats_result.png`): the "Job title" and "Company" labels and inputs are mis-aligned and run into each other.
   - "Skills asked for" lists "Cloud" and "Apache" as skills but not **Java** itself (Java does appear later in the keyword chips).
   - The "Job link" tab appears and disappears between visits.
   - The ATS scan counter ("20 of 20") vanishes after a scan instead of showing 19.
7. **Polish - noisy dashboard**:
   - The Tara chat opens by itself over the page. The dashboard also shows "60 messages left today", credits and "Talk to our team" all at once.
   - For an impatient user: one big "Upload resume" button and one "Check against a job" button are enough. Let Tara stay closed until clicked.
8. **Polish - a 404 on `/api/partner` on every workspace load**. A customer is not a partner, so it should not be called.

## 5. Scores (out of 10)
- First impression: **7**. Clear headline and up-front price. Lost points for the 429 blank page and the console errors.
- Look and feel (UI): **7**. Modern and tidy. The dashboard has too many cards; the ATS confirm card is mis-aligned.
- Ease of use (UX): **6**. Upload-to-builder is genuinely smooth. Too many parallel entry points (Improve / Upload / Tailor / ATS / Tara).
- Navigation: **7**. The sidebar is clear.
- Speed: **7**. The ATS scan in 2 s is excellent. The upload took up to about 30 s to confirm. Rate limiting hurts.
- Trust: **6**. "Guidance, not a real employer ATS result" is honest. The flipping "Preview" plan label and the dubious requirement verdicts cost trust.
- Value for money: **7**. ₹99 one download / ₹299 Job Hunt is cheap next to Jobscan (~US$50/month) and Zety. Only worth it if the match report gets smarter.
- Mobile: not tested.

## 6. Competitors I would normally use
- **Naukri (Resume Quality Score / free resume tools)**
  - Better than CVTara: I am already on Naukri, so recruiters see my profile there, and the score feeds straight into job applications.
  - CVTara does better: it actually checks against a *specific* JD, shows which requirements are evidenced, and builds a tailored copy. Naukri's score is generic.
- **Jobscan**
  - Better than CVTara: a mature skill-synonym taxonomy (it would not call NoSQL missing with MongoDB present), a hard-skills/soft-skills split, recruiter tips per ATS (Workday, Taleo), and a LinkedIn optimiser.
  - CVTara does better: far cheaper (₹299 vs about US$50/month), INR pricing and UPI, and an integrated builder and cover letter in one place. Its honest "guidance, not an ATS result" wording is also better than Jobscan's implied precision.
- **Resume Worded**
  - Better than CVTara: line-by-line bullet feedback (impact, quantification, weak verbs) and a LinkedIn review.
  - CVTara does better: JD-specific gap list and one-click tailored copy, at Indian prices.

## 7. Would I pay?
Maybe, **Job Hunt (₹299)**, but not today. I would pay sooner if:
- The requirement matcher stopped flagging things my resume obviously proves, so the score feels right to an engineer.
- The parser kept my LinkedIn URL and title.
- I could see the tailored resume and download in under 3 clicks after the report.
₹99 for one clean download is a fair impulse buy.

## 8. Is this the best site for me?
**Not yet.** The upload and parse and the speed are good. The JD match is the one thing I came for, and it is half-right: green ticks on every keyword next to "needs your review" on the same requirements. As a developer I trust a tool less when it contradicts itself. I would use Naukri (free) plus Jobscan's free scan to cross-check.

## 9. Top 5 changes
1. Fix the requirement matcher:
   - Use the found keywords as evidence for requirement bullets.
   - Add a synonym/umbrella map (NoSQL, CI/CD, Cloud).
   - Ignore intro/"About" lines.
   - Do not match vendor names inside certification titles.
2. Show a friendly 429 page and loosen per-IP limits for page loads; cut the ~20 API calls per dashboard load.
3. After import, update the profile (field, title, years) from the resume. Fix the LinkedIn URL truncation and pick up the title line.
4. Make the plan badge come from one source of truth. Never show "Preview" to a paid user because of a failed call.
5. Simplify the dashboard for returning users to two buttons: Upload/Improve and Check against a job. Keep Tara closed until clicked.

## 10. Security
| Attempt | Result |
|---|---|
| Customer calls `/api/admin/overview`, `/api/admin/purchases`, `/api/admin/settings` | **403** "You do not have access". Blocked |
| Open `/owner`, `/console`, `/owner.html`, `/console.html`, `/admin` as a customer | 404 "Page not available". `/admin` redirects to `/console`, which showed only "Loading". No data seen. Blocked |
| Read other users' records: random UUID on `/api/resumes/{id}`, `/api/ats/scans/{id}`, `/api/letters/{id}` | 404. Ids are UUIDs, so they cannot be guessed (no sequential ids to walk). Blocked |
| Export someone else's resume: `POST /export/resume/{random-uuid}/pdf {clean:true}` | 404. Blocked |
| Read another tester's data by query param: `?owner_id=27` on `/api/profile` and `/api/resumes/journey` | Param ignored, only my own data (owner 28) returned. Blocked |
| Mass assignment: `POST /api/profile` with extra `plan:"pro", role:"admin", is_admin:1, owner_id:27` | 200 OK, but the extra fields were ignored: `/api/allowance` still `job-hunt`, admin still 403. Blocked |
| Claim referral reward with no referrals: `POST /api/referral-rewards/claim {milestone:5}` | 422 "Not enough qualified friends yet". Blocked |
| XSS in ATS company field: `<img src=x onerror=alert(1)>Acme Bank` | Shown as plain text in the report and Recent checks. No dialog fired. Safe |
| XSS in profile name: `Rahul <img src=x onerror=alert(7)> D` | Dashboard greeting showed "Hi Rahul". No dialog fired. Safe |
| Response headers | Good: CSP (no `unsafe-eval`, but `script-src` allows `'unsafe-inline'`), HSTS, X-Frame-Options SAMEORIGIN, nosniff, httpOnly device cookie, CSRF token required |
| Non-resume file upload | Not tested (out of time). The upload input does restrict file types to pdf/docx/doc/txt/md |

**No security finding worked.** Small notes:
- `script-src 'unsafe-inline'` weakens the CSP.
- Purchase JSON returned to the customer includes internal fields: `provider_order_id`, `payment_reference: "manual_qa12"`, `limits_json`, `is_live`. These are not secret, but they should be trimmed.
- The customer JS bundle calls `/api/ai/status`. The name is not shown on screen, but it does mention "AI" in a public file.

## 11. What I did not like and who does it better
- **Requirement match logic**: Jobscan does this better with a skills taxonomy and synonyms. Copy that, and beat it on price and on the honest wording.
- **Dashboard clutter and the auto-opening chat**: Rezi and Resume Worded land you on one clear action ("Upload resume" / "Score my resume"). Copy that.
- **Bullet-level feedback**: Resume Worded tells you which bullet is weak and why. CVTara's ATS-friendly tips ("add phone", "summary 25-80 words") are generic. Add per-bullet impact feedback.
- **Profile and field from resume**: Naukri pre-fills experience, title and skills from the upload and asks to confirm. CVTara kept me as a "fresher Data Analyst".
- **Outage and rate-limit page**: every serious competitor serves a branded error page. CVTara's blank white 429 is the worst thing I saw.
