# t06: Mrs. Kulkarni, placement officer (partner manager), laptop

Tested on 2026-11-13, 04:24 to 04:40 UTC, against live https://cvtara.com. I used Playwright (Chromium) at 1366x860, plus one iPhone 13 check of /partners.html. I looked at every screenshot before taking the next step. Screenshots are in `tests/live/results/agents/t06/`.

## 1. Who I am and what I came to do
I am the Training & Placement Officer of an engineering college with 400 final-year students. I came to decide whether CVTara is worth bringing to my principal as the college's resume and interview tool before the placement season. I also wanted to see what my login as a partner manager gives me.

## 2. First 10 seconds
- **Homepage (`01-home.png`):** it is clean and clearly a consumer resume site ("₹99 to download one resume", Diwali Dhamaka banner). **Nothing on the homepage, top menu, footer or About page mentions colleges, placement cells or partners.** I could only find `/partners.html` through the sitemap. A real TPO who arrives from Google or a WhatsApp forward of the homepage would conclude "this is for individual students" and leave.
- **/partners.html (`02`, `03`):** once found, it is honest and calm. It explains what students get, how the partnership works and what you do not promise, and it has a short form. I felt it was trustworthy but thin. It shows no college logos, no numbers, no sample report, no price or price range, no "book a 15-minute demo", no phone or WhatsApp number, and no named person. A principal will ask me "who else uses it and what does it cost?", and this page cannot answer either question.
- **Would I stay?** Yes, long enough to fill the form, because it is short. I would not convince my principal with this page alone.

## 4. Problems (most serious first)
1. **The college offer is invisible.** *Major (business blocker).* Where: homepage nav and footer, /about.html; only sitemap.xml links /partners.html. There is no "For colleges" link anywhere, and /partners, /colleges and /for-colleges all return 404. Fix: add "For colleges" to the top nav and footer, add a strip on the homepage, and 301 /partners, /colleges and /for-colleges to /partners.html. Screenshot: `01-home.png`.
2. **The portal is an affiliate dashboard, not a placement-cell report.** *Major.* Where: Workspace > Partner portal (`11-portal-view-full.png`).
   - **Missing:** everything a TPO needs. There is no count of students who finished a resume, no average ATS match, no interview-practice completion, no "who has not started" nudge list (counts only is fine), no batch or branch split, and no PDF/Excel report to show the principal.
   - **Shown instead:** commission %, "Revenue", "Paid to you", "Request payout … against your invoice", and a league table of partners.
   - **Promise not delivered:** the partners page promises "a report of how your students are doing … before placement season". The portal does not deliver that.
   - **Commission framing:** many government and aided colleges cannot take commission from students' purchases. That framing is a reputational risk for a TPO ("you are earning from our students?").
   - Fix: give colleges a separate view with readiness metrics and an export, and hide commission and payout unless the agreement is a revenue-share one.
3. **No price, proof or demo on /partners.html.** *Major.* There are no seat prices or ranges, no partner logos, no case study, no sample report screenshot, no demo booking and no phone. Superset shows 600+ colleges, named IIT/IIM/BITS logos, ISO 27001, a phone number and "Get Free Demo". Fix: add an indicative per-student price (for example "from ₹X per student per season"), one sample report image, a Calendly-style demo slot and a named contact.
4. **The student invite landing is not branded.** *Major.* `/p/<code>?i=<token>` goes to the generic /register (`14-landing.png`). Students get no sign of the college, the seat or that it is free for them, and the invited email is not pre-filled. Students will think it is spam or a paid upsell. Fix: show "Invited by <College> placement cell: your seat is free", pre-fill the email and hide the pricing upsell for seat holders.
5. **Invite counters and messages are wrong.** *Minor.*
   - "4 personal links made" but only 3 links were made; the existing-account row was counted as "added".
   - The campaign line then shows "4 invited", which overstates it.
   - A row with no email was dropped silently, and the response still says "unreadable: 0".
   - The campaign stats do not refresh after "Make personal links" until reload.
   - There is no on-screen list of invited students, only a CSV download.

   Fix: count only links actually created, report skipped rows, re-render after the call and list the invites in a table.
6. **The deep link `?view=partner` does not work.** *Minor.* `/workspace.html?view=partner` lands on Dashboard. Other views may behave the same. I would bookmark this link, and partner emails would use it. Fix: handle the view parameter for dynamically added views (the portal button is injected after the /api/partner call).
7. **The workspace is heavy and slow.** *Minor (speed).*
   - It loads **43 separate JS files** and about 30 API calls on every load, with /api/resumes ×4, /api/allowance ×4 and /api/profile ×3.
   - It took 8 s after sign-in, and one navigation timed out at 30 s waiting for "load".
   - A file is literally named `fomo-popup.js`, which is not a good look if a principal's IT person views the source.

   Fix: bundle the scripts, remove duplicate calls and lazy-load per view.
8. **A partner manager sees the student UI.** *Minor.* After login I see "Your resume is one click away", "You're on the Free plan", "See plans" and an auto-opening Tara chat. "Partner portal" is buried under "Create & Improve" between Interview practice and Career assistance, with a plain dot icon. Fix: for partner managers, land on the portal, put it in its own sidebar group and do not auto-open Tara.
9. **Consumer CTAs and festive banner on the B2B page.** *Polish.* /partners.html on mobile shows the "Diwali Dhamaka" banner and a sticky "Check free / View plans" bar (`19-partners-mobile.png`). Fix: hide both on /partners.html, or replace the bar with "Talk to us".
10. **Inconsistent branding.** *Polish.*
    - The homepage, login and workspace use the stylised "CVTara" wordmark with the CV-and-star logo. /partners.html uses a different blue square icon with plain "CVTara" text (`02` vs `01`).
    - The homepage says "Built in Bangalore by an HR professional", while the partners page and footer say "an HR team".
    - In the portal, the bright lime "Make personal links" button clashes with the blue/teal brand.
    - The file input is truncated to "No f…osen".
11. **Weak form confirmation.** *Polish.* After sending, the only feedback is one line of small grey text in the middle of the form. There is no reference number, no "we have emailed you a copy" and no next step (for example "meanwhile, here is a sample report").
12. **Bad partner codes redirect silently.** *Polish.* `/p/does-not-exist` silently goes to the homepage. A student given a mistyped link gets no explanation.
13. **Misleading error for a reward that does not exist.** *Polish.* `/api/partner/rewards/99999/address` answers 409 "This record changed. Reload before saving." It should be 404.

## 6. Compared with competitors
- **Superset (joinsuperset.com):**
  - Gives that CVTara does not: 600+ colleges, named IIT/IIM/BITS customers, ISO 27001 and a phone number with "Get Free Demo". It is a full placement workflow (drives, employers, student records) with mobile apps and per-student analytics.
  - CVTara does better: deeper resume, ATS-against-a-job-post, cover-letter and interview practice for the student.
- **Big Interview (700+ US universities):**
  - Gives that CVTara does not: an admin dashboard where career staff **assign** practice interviews and resume tasks, track completion and scores, and export usage reports. That is exactly what my portal lacks.
  - CVTara does better: Indian context, rupee pricing, and a builder with Indian role examples.
- **VMock / Workruit / Resumod (India):**
  - Gives that CVTara does not: Workruit and Resumod offer college-branded templates and university brochures (Resumod claims 154 universities); VMock is a benchmarked resume score many campuses already know.
  - CVTara does better: a clean privacy stance ("we never share a student's resume without consent"), no auto-renewal, and simple sign-up links with no account creation by the college.

## 7. Would I pay?
Not yet. I would sign a **free pilot for one batch** (for example 60 students in one branch) if CVTara gave me a per-student readiness report and a branded invite page. I would pay sooner with:
- a clear per-student seat price (₹100–200 per student per season would be easy to approve)
- a sample report PDF
- one reference college I can call

I would **not** accept a commission arrangement on students' purchases; my college cannot be seen earning from students.

## 8. Is this the best site for me?
**No, not today.** The student tools look good, and the privacy stance is better than most. But as a placement officer I cannot find the offer, cannot see a price, and cannot show my principal a progress report. The portal measures CVTara's sales through my students rather than my students' readiness.

## 9. Top 5 changes
1. Add "For colleges" to the main nav, footer and homepage, and redirect /partners, /colleges and /for-colleges.
2. Build a college report in the portal: invited → signed up → resume finished → ATS check done → interview practice done, by batch or branch, with a PDF/Excel export. Hide commission and payouts for seat-based colleges.
3. Put proof, a price range and a "book a demo" option on /partners.html, including a screenshot of the sample report.
4. Brand the student invite landing with the college name and "your seat is free", and pre-fill the invited email.
5. Fix the invite counts and add an on-screen invite list. Fix the `?view=partner` deep link, and land partner managers on their portal instead of the student dashboard.

## 10. Security
| Attempt | Result |
|---|---|
| HTML/script in partner application name and message (`<b>`, `<img onerror>`, `<script>`) | Accepted and stored (id 1). No dialog fired on my side. **The owner should check that the console shows it as plain text**; I could not see the console |
| HTML in a student name in invites (`<img src=x onerror=alert(9)>`) | The portal escapes all output (the `e()` escape function is used everywhere in partner-portal.js); no dialog fired. Safe |
| CSV/formula injection (`=HYPERLINK(...)`) in an invite name | The download CSV prefixes `'` (`'=HYPERLINK…`). Safe |
| Invite without the consent tick, through the API (`authority:0`) | 422. Blocked |
| Invite into another partner's campaign (`linkId:2`, `999`) | 404. Blocked |
| `GET /api/partner?partner_id=2`, `/api/partner/2`, `/api/partners`, `/api/partners/1` | Parameter ignored (returns my own partner only); others 404. Blocked |
| `GET /api/partner/invites?link_id=2` | Returns only my invites. Blocked |
| Payout with ₹0 balance (`POST /api/partner/payout`, also with `amountPaise:500000`) | 422 "Payout can be requested once ₹5,000 is ready to pay. You have ₹0." Blocked; the amount from the client is ignored |
| Accept agreement id 1 or 99999 with a fake hash | 404. Blocked |
| Change delivery address on reward ids 1, 2, 99999 | 409 "This record changed" (no change made). Blocked, but the error message is misleading (problem 13) |
| Uploading a non-spreadsheet as the invite file (`sheet` = random base64) | 422 "That file could not be read". Safe |
| `/api/admin/settings`, `overview`, `purchases`, `partners`, `partner-applications`, `letter-summary` as partner manager | All 403. Blocked |
| `/workspace.html` without login (curl) | Redirects to /login. Good |
| `/p/<script>…` and an `i=` token containing `"><svg onload>` | 403 Forbidden from the firewall. Blocked |
| **Account check through invites** | **Worked (low/medium privacy finding).** A partner manager can paste any email into "Invite your students" and learn whether it has a CVTara account ("Already has a CVTara account"; status `has_account` in `/api/partner/invites`). Steps: Partner portal → Or paste → `Name, someone@domain` → tick consent → Make personal links. A partner can probe any list of emails this way (I used only a QA tester's address). Fix: do not reveal account status per email. Say "links made for all rows", and have the server simply not count existing accounts |
| Information exposure | The customer workspace bundle `owner-public.js` names admin endpoints (`/api/admin/settings`, `/overview`, `/purchases`, `/letter-summary`). All are 403, so this is not exploitable, but it maps the admin API for attackers. Low |

**Nothing serious worked.** Authorization on partner data is solid. The one real finding is the account-existence check through invites.

## 11. What I did not like, and who does it better
- **Discoverability:** Superset and Big Interview have a dedicated "For universities" section in the main nav with "Get a demo". CVTara should copy this.
- **Proof:** Superset shows college logos, 600+ count, ISO 27001, press and a phone number. CVTara should add even 2–3 pilot colleges, a named contact and a phone or WhatsApp number.
- **Admin control:** Big Interview lets staff assign tasks and track completion per student with exports. CVTara should let a TPO assign "finish resume by 15 Dec" and see completion counts.
- **Branding for the college:** Workruit offers college-selected, college-branded templates. CVTara should allow a college logo on the invite page and an optional "college format" template.
- **Pricing clarity:** none of the B2B competitors show price either, but CVTara's whole consumer brand is "clear ₹ prices, no auto-renewal". Showing an indicative per-student seat price would beat all of them.
- **Commission language:** an affiliate framing ("Paid to you", rankings against other partners) suits YouTubers and coaching institutes, not a college placement cell. Split the portal by partner type.
