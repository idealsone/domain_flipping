I am Arjun, a final-year B.E. computer science student in campus placement season, using my phone. I came to do a full mock interview for a fresher software role, get help from Tara, track 3 applications (Infosys, TCS, Wipro), and see whether refer-and-earn is worth sharing with my batch.

## 2. First 10 seconds
The home page (`01_home.png`) is clear: "One good resume, and a sharper copy for every job you apply to", with the price (₹99) and "nothing renews" shown at the top. It loads in about 2 s, looks clean and feels Indian and honest, and I would stay. But the page is all about resumes and never mentions interview practice, the thing I came for. On a phone the hero is very tall, the second CTA is hidden behind the sticky "Check free / View plans" bar, and I had to scroll to find anything about interviews.

## 4. Problems (most serious first)
1. **BLOCKER: no interview roles for engineering or IT freshers.** Page: workspace, Interview practice, "Start a mock interview", "Find your role". There are only 24 roles, none of them software, IT, engineering, graduate trainee or campus fresher. Searching "software engineer" shows "No match yet" (`06_role_search.png`). For the largest group of Indian job seekers, B.E./B.Tech freshers in placement season, the paid feature (4 mocks on Job Hunt) does not exist. Even Tara admits it. Fix: add a Software Engineer / Graduate Engineer Trainee / Campus fresher (HR + aptitude + project) role, and hide or caveat "4 mock interviews" on pricing for roles that are not covered.
2. **MAJOR (test setup or entitlement bug): the Job Hunt grant does not show.** The brief says this account has a ₹0 Job Hunt test grant, but the dashboard, Tara and `/api/allowance` all say Free with mockInterviews 0. Either the grant was never applied, or granted plans are not read. If a real customer's paid or comped plan can fail like this silently, that is a serious support risk. Fix: check the purchases row for user 31, and show "Plan: X until date" in Account.
3. **MAJOR: the "Ask Tara" floating button covers main buttons on the phone.** It covers "Add your first application" (`11_tracker_empty.png`), "Share on WhatsApp" on Referrals (`17_refer.png`) and the stats cards (`05_interview.png`). Fix: add bottom padding to the page, collapse the button to an icon on mobile, or hide it while scrolling.
4. **MAJOR: Tara gives wrong account facts.** It said there was no mock-interview count on my account, and that ATS scans left was 0 while the dashboard showed 0/3 used today. An assistant that gets the user's own plan wrong destroys trust. Fix: feed it the same allowance numbers the page shows.
5. **MAJOR: the workspace is slow on mobile.** It took about 9-10 s to DOMContentLoaded on the iPhone profile, and one load timed out at 25 s, while the home page loads in about 2 s. The workspace page loads about 20 CSS files plus many scripts. Fix: bundle them, and load the console CSS (`owner-console.css`) only for staff.
6. **MINOR: misleading error when a security filter blocks input.** Saving a tracker entry that contained a script-like string returned 403 with "The service is unavailable. Keep your draft and retry." (`13_form_still_open_3.png`). A real user who pastes odd text from a job ad will retry forever. Fix: say "Some characters in your notes were not accepted" and name the field.
7. **MINOR: US date format** (mm/dd/yyyy in the form and "Follow up · 11/25/2026" on cards). Indians read 11/05 as 11 May. Fix: show "25 Nov 2026".
8. **MINOR: the tracker kanban scrolls sideways on the phone** and you can't see the stages at a glance (`16_tracker_cards.png`). Fix: show a stacked list with a stage chip on mobile, like Huntr's mobile list.
9. **MINOR: "The job url field must be a valid URL."** is raw developer wording. Fix: "Paste the full job link, starting with https://".
10. **POLISH:** page titles are cut off ("Interview pr…", "Application …"). The practice questions shown to a fresher are about "repetitive work without errors". Silent sign-out with no "your session expired" message.
11. **POLISH:** the referral page counts my own logged-out visit to my own link as "1 visits from your link".

## 5. Scores (out of 10)
- First impression: 7. The home page is clean, honest and priced up front, but interview practice is not visible.
- Look and feel (UI): 7. Calm, readable cards, but the floating Tara button covers content and the cards are heavy.
- Ease of use (UX): 5. The tracker form is fine, but my main job (a mock interview) was impossible.
- Navigation: 6. The menu is clear, but there are too many duplicate entries (Account twice, Billing twice).
- Speed: 4. The home page is fast; the workspace takes about 10 s on the phone.
- Trust: 6. Honest wording ("does not predict any interview result"), but Tara got my account wrong and my plan did not match what I was told.
- Value for money: 3 for me. I would pay for mocks that don't cover my role.
- Mobile: 5. It works, but there is the overlapping bubble, the sideways board and the slow load.

## 6. Competitors I would actually use
- **Naukri (Naukri 360 / campus) / Unstop / PrepInsta**: they have TCS NQT, Infosys and Wipro aptitude, coding and HR mocks for freshers, often free or cheap. CVTara has no technical or campus track at all. CVTara is better on calm, honest resume tailoring and on no auto-renew.
- **Google Interview Warmup (free)**: practice questions by job field, including IT support and data, with spoken answers and instant feedback on what you said. CVTara's mock is written-only and paywalled. CVTara is better at tying the mock to a resume and a tracker in one place.
- **Huntr / Teal (tracker)**: Chrome clipper, a mobile-friendly list, and contacts and tasks per job. CVTara's tracker is basic but has reminders and links resume versions, which is nice for one place.
- Tara chat compared with free ChatGPT-style tools: Tara is honest and context-aware, but it is capped at 10 messages a day and made a factual error about my own plan.

## 7. Would I pay?
Not today. Job Hunt at ₹299 promises 4 mock interviews that can't be used for my role. I would pay ₹299 sooner if it had (a) a Software Engineer / GET campus mock with HR, project and basic technical questions, (b) voice answers on the phone, and (c) a student price or a referral discount I could use myself (₹50 off on ₹299 via a friend's link already helps).

## 8. Is this the best site for me?
No. For a B.E. student in placement season, the interview part does not cover my job, and that is what I came for. For a clean resume plus a simple application tracker, it is decent and more honest than most.

## 9. Top 5 changes first
1. Add engineering, IT and campus-fresher roles to interview practice (and a role search that maps "software engineer" to them).
2. Fix the plan/grant display. Account should show the active plan and its end date, and Tara must read the same numbers.
3. Make the Ask Tara bubble icon-only on mobile and add bottom padding so it never covers buttons.
4. Cut the workspace load on the phone to under 3 s (bundle CSS/JS, skip staff CSS for customers).
5. Use Indian date format, plain-English form errors, and a stacked tracker list on mobile.

## 11. What I did not like and who does it better
- **Interview coverage**: Unstop, PrepInsta and Naukri campus mocks cover TCS, Infosys, Wipro and Accenture fresher rounds. CVTara should copy that with a "Campus placement" track (HR + project + aptitude).
- **Answer mode**: Google Interview Warmup lets you speak your answer and shows what you said. Typing long answers on a phone is painful, so CVTara should add voice-to-text.
- **Assistant accuracy**: Kickresume and Rezi assistants don't claim account facts. If Tara does, it must read the live allowance.
- **Tracker on mobile**: Huntr and Teal show a clean list with stage chips and dates like "25 Nov". CVTara should beat them by keeping its reminders and resume-version link.
- **Referral sharing**: CRED and Groww show a large sticky WhatsApp share button. CVTara's WhatsApp button is half-hidden behind the Tara bubble.
- **Speed**: Canva and Resume.io dashboards open in about 2-3 s on mobile. CVTara's workspace takes about 10 s.
