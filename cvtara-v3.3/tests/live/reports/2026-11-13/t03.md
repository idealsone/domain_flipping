orange "Diwali Dhamaka 2026" banner on top feels like a discount shop, not a career site. The sample resume is a software

to load and the workspace after sign-in took **30 s** to show (later 11 s) — a real person would think it is broken.

## 4. Problems (most serious first)
1. **Major — Tailoring barely moves the score** (dashboard → "Resume + cover letter for a job", `22-run-14.png`). ATS match 32% → **35%** after tailoring. For a career changer that is demoralising: I paid for "Job Hunt" to be told I'm at 35%. It explains why (PF/ESI, VLOOKUP missing) — honest, good — but it should offer to add the things I *do* have and show what a realistic target score is. Fix: show "what would raise it" with one-click add buttons; explain that 35% for a career changer is normal.
2. **Major — Tailor says my languages are missing when they are on my resume.** I entered "English, Kannada, Tamil, Hindi" in Languages (visible in preview, `13-xss-preview.png`), yet the check says "The job asks for English and Kannada/Hindi. Add the languages you speak". Either the Languages section isn't read by the matcher or it's a stale copy. Fix: include all sections in the match.
3. **Major — Speed.** Workspace took 30 s to DOMContentLoaded once, 11 s another time; `whats-new.js` returned **502**, `/api/applications` returned **502** once. Low-tech users will leave. Session also expired after ~3 h and silently showed the login page.
4. **Major — Wrong pre-filled job title "Data Analyst"** in the builder (`09-editor.png`) after I had chosen HR Executive in the picker. Looks like it came from the template sample or an old profile value. Fix: use the role chosen in step 2.
5. **Major — "Payment confirmed" ticked while "Payment pending" unticked** on a ₹0 test grant (`22-run-14.png`). The word "payment" at all, on a plan I already have, scares a cautious buyer. Fix: for plan holders show "Included in your Job Hunt plan".
6. **Minor — Plan shows "Preview / See plans" on first dashboard load** (`03-after-login.png`) then "Job Hunt 30 days left". Looks like I lost my plan.
7. **Minor — Tara chat pops open uninvited** over the builder (`04-builder.png`) and greets me as "Hi QA!" (from account name — fine for QA, but greeting by first name from a placeholder is risky for real users who type odd names).
8. **Minor — Dates shown as "2012-06"** in the resume preview (`12-experience.png`), with no "– Present" when the end date is empty. Indian recruiters expect "Jun 2012 – Present". Also the "I currently work here" box is easy to miss.
9. **Minor — The figure check flagged "600"** in the letter as "not in your resume" — 600 is the company size from the job ad, so it's a false alarm, but good that it checks numbers.
10. **Polish — Picker lists both "Transport & Logistics" and "Transportation"**; "Profession" filter has "HR & People" in a hidden `<option>` while the visible chips say "Human Resources" — two names for the same thing.
11. **Polish — Draft name stays "Untitled resume (main)"** in the tailor dropdown. Ask for a name on first save.

## 5. Scores (out of 10)
- First impression: **7** — clear promise, trustworthy HR founder line; Diwali banner cheapens it.
- Look and feel (UI): **7** — clean, calm, good live preview; dashboard is crowded with three overlapping "next step" boxes after onboarding.
- Ease of use (UX): **6** — guided picker and section-by-section builder are genuinely easy; finding the tailor tool and the download is not.
- Navigation: **5** — `?view=` links don't work for ATS, dashboard changes content between visits, tiles vanish.
- Speed: **3** — 6–30 s waits and 502s.
- Trust: **6** — honest "check before you send" warnings are excellent; "payment" steps on a paid plan and plan label flicker hurt.
- Value for money: **6** — ₹99 single download is fair for India; but a 3-point ATS lift won't convince me Job Hunt is worth it.
- Mobile: not used.

## 6. Competitors I'd normally use
- **Naukri (Resume Display / Naukri FastForward)**: I already have a Naukri profile; recruiters search there. CVTara can't push my profile to recruiters. CVTara is better: real per-job tailoring + cover letter + consistency check, which Naukri doesn't do.
- **Canva**: free, beautiful templates, instant PDF download, no waiting. CVTara is better: ATS-safe layouts, guided content for career changers, job matching.
- **Zety / Resume.io**: smoother builder with pre-written bullet suggestions per job title ("HR Executive" phrases one click away) and instant download; but ₹/$ trial traps. CVTara is better: honest Indian pricing (₹99 one-off, refund line on home page) and it warns you not to invent skills.
- **Jobscan**: shows a full keyword table with counts and lets you re-scan after editing. CVTara shows only a sentence-level summary and a small score change.

## 7. Would I pay?
Yes, ₹99 for one resume download, because the builder is easy and the letter is checked against my resume. I would not
buy Job Hunt yet: the tailoring only moved me 3 points and I couldn't see the download in the time I had. I'd pay sooner
if tailoring showed a clear before/after of my bullets and a download button right under the preview.

## 8. Is this the best site for me?
Mostly yes for a career changer in India: the "Career change → HR Executive" path is better than anything I'd find on
Canva or Naukri, and the warnings are honest. But it's slow and I got lost between the dashboard tiles; a less patient
teacher would give up before the cover letter.

## 9. Top 5 changes
1. Fix speed (30 s workspace load, 502s on `whats-new.js` and `/api/applications`).
2. Put "Download resume" and "Download cover letter" buttons directly in the tailor preview, and hide payment steps for plan holders.
3. Make the matcher read every section (languages) and show a keyword table with "add this" buttons, so tailoring lifts the score visibly.
4. Use the role chosen in the picker; never pre-fill "Data Analyst"; ask for a draft name.
5. Don't auto-open Tara; keep one "next step" box on the dashboard, not three.

## 10. Security
- **Stored XSS in builder:** put `<img src=x onerror=alert(1)><script>alert("t03")</script><b>Best Teacher Award 2019</b>` into "Awards & volunteering", saved to profile. Preview showed it as plain text; no `img[src=x]`, no script, no `<b>` element created; no alert dialog. **Blocked (good).** This text then went into the tailor run; no alert there either.
- **Admin/API probe as customer:** `GET /api/me` → 404 "This action is not available." (no data leak). `/api/partner` 404 for a customer (expected).
- Not done (ran out of time): cross-account id swapping, download-without-paying, coupon reuse, upload of non-resume file. Nothing serious found in what I tried.

## 11. What I did not like and who does it better
- **Slow loading** — Canva and Resume.io open the editor in 2–3 s. Copy: lazy-load the template gallery and Tara; don't block the page on `whats-new.js`.
- **Hidden download** — Zety/Kickresume keep a permanent "Download" button top-right of the editor. CVTara should do the same in the tailor preview.
- **Weak tailoring feedback** — Jobscan gives a keyword-by-keyword table (found/missing, count). CVTara should show that table and let me add a missing skill in one click with a "only if true" tick.
- **No bullet suggestions** — Zety/Resume.io offer ready phrases per job title. For a teacher moving to HR, "suggested HR-relevant bullets from teaching work" would be the killer feature CVTara is closest to.
- **Festive banner** — Naukri and LinkedIn don't shout sales on career tools; a quiet "Diwali offer" pill would feel more professional.
- **Brand/greeting** — "Hi QA!" style first-name greeting from account name; Kickresume asks your preferred name first.
