CTC to in-hand salary guide

How is in-hand salary calculated from CTC in India? In-hand = CTC minus employer contributions (PF 12% on basic) minus your deductions (employee PF, professional tax, TDS). PF is capped at 12% of ₹15,000 basic. Your actual payslip may differ.

Components of CTC

  • Basic salary — usually 40–50% of CTC; basis for PF calculation
  • HRA — house rent allowance; partially tax-exempt if in a metro
  • Special allowance — taxable; often used to fill up to the CTC figure
  • Employer PF — 12% of basic, capped at ₹15,000 basic
  • Gratuity — actuarial provision, part of CTC but paid only after 5 years
  • Variable / performance bonus — may or may not pay out

Typical deductions

  • Employee PF — 12% of basic (max ₹1,800/month)
  • Professional tax — varies by state, ~₹200/month in Karnataka/Maharashtra
  • TDS — income tax withheld per your declared regime and investments

Example

₹6 lakh CTC with 40% basic = ₹2.4 lakh basic. PF capped at ₹15,000 so employee PF = ₹1,800/month = ₹21,600/year. After PF and professional tax deductions, before TDS, gross in-hand is roughly ₹4.6 lakh/year or ~₹38,000/month. TDS reduces this further based on your tax regime and investments.

This is illustrative. Use for orientation, not for salary declarations, bank statements or tax filing.
