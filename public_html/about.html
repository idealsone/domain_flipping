About

Built to help you write a resume you can defend in an interview.

CVTara is a career workspace built by an HR professional working in the edtech industry. The goal is one thing: help Indian job seekers prepare clear resumes and more focused applications — without inventing experience they cannot back up.

Why it exists

Most resumes are rejected before a human reads them.

A parsing failure, a missing keyword, or a layout that hides your strongest line. CVTara addresses that — not by writing a story for you, but by showing you the gap and giving you a text-first layout that a portal can actually read.

What it does not do

CVTara does not invent experience.

Profile optimization reframes lines you already entered. It does not add employers, dates, tools or results you did not provide. The HR Analytics Engine compares text you paste — it cannot see design, photographs or a hiring manager's judgment.

Payment

Card numbers stay at Razorpay.

CVTara stores a payment reference and access dates. It does not store card numbers, and it does not claim certifications it has not earned. A pass starts after the payment provider confirms the transaction.

Feedback

Published reviews are owner-approved.

Notes submitted from the workspace or the feedback page go to the owner queue first. They appear on the homepage only after the owner reviews and publishes them. Nothing appears automatically.
