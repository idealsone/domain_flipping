Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026. This page describes the public-site practice.

What we collect on this public site

The marketing pages store a theme preference in your browser. The in-browser job comparison runs on your device and is not uploaded until you create an account and choose to save.

Accounts and resumes

When the workspace is live, an account stores the name, email, career stage and resume drafts you save. You can request deletion through the contact addresses on the contact page.

Payments

Card numbers are entered on Razorpay. CVTara stores payment references and access dates, not card PANs. CVTara does not claim PCI-DSS certification — Razorpay is the certified payment processor.

Marketing

Marketing email is sent only after explicit opt-in. Every message includes an unsubscribe link. CVTara does not buy or scrape email lists.
