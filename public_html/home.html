Questions

Direct answers, no marketing fog.

Is the ATS scan really free?

Yes. Upload a PDF or Word file, or paste the text. Add the job as a listing link or as the full description. You get a score, keyword gap list, and issue breakdown in seconds — no account needed.

What does the score mean?

The score measures keyword overlap between your resume and the job description, plus action-verb density. It is not a hiring decision or a ranking. A higher score means more of the job's language appears in your resume. Full explanation →

Can I preview templates without creating an account?

Yes. Click any template card on this page or the Templates page. A full A4 preview opens with fictional sample content. You see exactly how the layout looks before writing a single word.

What is ATS-friendly?

Standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills), selectable text in PDF, no important content inside images or tables, standard fonts. Full guide →

Does CVTara write experience for me?

No. Suggested wording reframes lines you already entered. CVTara never adds a company, a date, a tool, or a result you didn't provide. Every suggestion is labeled so you know what changed.

How does payment work?

Checkout is hosted by the payment provider. Card numbers are entered on their page — not on CVTara. CVTara stores only a payment reference and the access start date. Your workspace opens after the payment is verified.

What do I get after I pay?

A workspace with 50+ templates, cover letters, watermark-free export, job-matched wording and an application tracker. Preview and scan stay free.
