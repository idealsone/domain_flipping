Refund policy

Refund requests are reviewed against unused access time and verified payment records. Contact billing@cvtara.com with your payment reference.

Eligibility

Requests submitted within 7 days of a verified payment and before significant use of the workspace features are eligible for review. Chargebacks after a referral commission has been paid are held for 14 days before any payout is made.

How to request

Email billing@cvtara.com with your registered email and the Razorpay payment ID shown in your confirmation. We will reply within 5 business days.

Card numbers

CVTara does not store card numbers. Razorpay is the payment processor. For payment disputes, include your Razorpay order reference.
