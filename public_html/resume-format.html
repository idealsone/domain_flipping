Resume format for a job

What is the best resume format for a job in India? Reverse chronological order, single-column layout, PDF, standard fonts at 10–12pt. One page for freshers; two pages for five or more years of experience.

PDF vs DOCX

Send PDF unless the JD says DOCX. PDF renders identically on every device and preserves spacing. DOCX can reflow on other Word versions and some fonts may substitute, shifting your layout.

One page or two?

Freshers and candidates with under five years of experience: one page. More than five years: two pages maximum. Never pad to fill the second page — recruiters notice empty bullets.

Section order

  1. Name and contact
  2. Summary (optional for experienced; recommended for freshers)
  3. Work Experience (reverse chronological)
  4. Education
  5. Skills
  6. Projects (if relevant)
  7. Certifications (if recent)

Fonts and size

Calibri, Arial, Times New Roman or Georgia at 10–12pt body and 14–16pt name. Line spacing 1.15 to 1.25. Enough white space so a recruiter can scan in 7 seconds.
