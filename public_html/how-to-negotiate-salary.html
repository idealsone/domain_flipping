How to negotiate salary

How do I negotiate salary in India? Wait for the written offer. Research market rates. Counter once with a specific number, a brief reason, and readiness to accept the revised offer. This is a guide — it does not guarantee any outcome.

When to negotiate

After you have a written offer in hand. Do not negotiate during the first round interview or before an offer is extended. If HR asks for your current CTC before an offer, you may give a range or decline politely.

Research the market rate

Check role + city + years of experience on Glassdoor, AmbitionBox or LinkedIn Salary. Use percentile mid-point, not the maximum, as your anchor unless you have rare skills.

How to counter

  1. Express enthusiasm for the role and company
  2. State your number once, with one reason: "Based on my experience in X and the market rate for this role in this city, I was expecting ₹ Y."
  3. Pause. Let the recruiter respond.
  4. If the gap is small, accept. If not, ask what the maximum band is and decide.

What not to do

  • Counter more than once in the same conversation
  • Mention personal expenses as a reason
  • Anchor very high unless your market data supports it
  • Negotiate by email if a phone call is possible
