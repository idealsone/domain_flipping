How to write a resume

How do I write a resume? Four steps: Pick a role and company → gather evidence (projects, numbers) → choose a single-column PDF format → compare with the job description and close keyword gaps.

Preview templatesScan a JD free

Step 1 — Pick a role before you write

A resume targeted at a role converts better than a general CV. Choose a title and a company before you start. Everything you include should answer: "Would this make a recruiter for that role say yes?"

Step 2 — Gather evidence first

List every project, outcome, number and tool from the past three years. You will filter this into bullet points, not invent new ones. Write what you can defend in an interview.

Step 3 — Choose the format

Use reverse chronological order (newest first). Single-column for ATS. Standard headings: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Projects. PDF unless the employer specifies DOCX. One or two pages depending on experience (freshers: one page).

Step 4 — Compare with the job description

Paste the job description and your draft into a comparison tool. Look for keywords in the JD that your resume doesn't mention. Add them where they appear naturally. Do not stuff.
