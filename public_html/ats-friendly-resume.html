ATS-friendly resume format for India

What makes a resume ATS-friendly? Standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills), selectable text (not images of text), single-column body for Work Experience, and no important words hidden in headers, footers or tables.

What ATS does

Applicant Tracking Systems extract text from your file to compare it with the job description. They cannot read text embedded in images, and some struggle with tables or multi-column layouts because columns are read left-to-right across a row, mixing the data.

Six ATS rules for Indian resumes

  1. Use standard heading names — Work Experience, Education, Skills, Projects, Certifications. Creative names like "My Journey" or "What I've Done" may not be parsed.
  2. Export as PDF with selectable text — not as a scanned image or picture.
  3. Keep Work Experience in a single column — company, title, date range, and bullets.
  4. No tables for bullet points — tables can cause mis-reading of dates and job titles.
  5. No important text in headers or footers — name and email belong in the body.
  6. Font size ≥ 10pt, standard typeface — Times New Roman, Calibri, Georgia. Decorative fonts may not extract correctly.

What ATS cannot check

Accuracy of your claims, quality of your writing, and whether your experience genuinely fits. ATS filtering is a first screen — the recruiter reads the CV that passes.
